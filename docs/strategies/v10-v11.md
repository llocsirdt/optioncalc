# v10 / v11 — "target engines" (backtest-only, NOT on the live roster)

No roster literal: v10 and v11 are **not rostered** (`index.js` builds no v10/v11 variant). They are a separate
engine, not a `runDay5m` signal family.

- Rules: `server/src/candle-spread/target-rules.js` — every v10/v11 decision (config, book state, open gates with
  their trace, anchored entry, fly, cap / book-reject, cover machinery, touches, floor) behind a **pricer**
  interface (`pr.now` / `pr.at` / `pr.flyAt`; `null` = cannot price, order left alone).
- Bar loop: `server/src/candle-spread/backtest/backtest-target.js` (`runDayTarget`): only the loop, 1m /
  inferred-path fills, RTH line builders and metrics.
- Sweep: `scripts/candle-spread/sweep-v10.js` (widths 10 / 20 / 40 / `sig` / `mix`; v6-v9 controls through
  `runDay5m` on the same days).
- Live trader `target-trader.js`: **not built.**

Shared machinery: the cover machinery is the ported `runDay5m` one (via `opts.coverCfg`), not the live trader's.
Triggers and the strategy × signal matrix: [signals.md](signals.md). Not to be confused with the old
"v10 = mean-reversion exits" research script (experiments log, parked).

## Intent

From the 2026-10-16 manual-trades study (`target-rules.js:7-10`):

- **v10 — hold toward a close target.** Lean a debit vertical (and, near the target, a fly) toward the /NQ 15m
  9EMA (the 60m 9EMA when it agrees, the NDX-RTH 60m EMA on a gap morning), short at the money, standing tent
  cover.
- **v11 — touch target.** Open toward the NDX-RTH 15m BB mid when the /NQ 15m **and** 60m trends both point to it,
  short part of the way (`f` 0.5), **cover at the touch**, give-up pricing on every cover from 15:00.

Signals from /NQ (last completed candles), pricing / fills / RTH lines / settlement from cash NDX; NQ levels move
into pricing space with the live basis. Width `sig` = 0.25 × the 1σ move rounded to the strike grid, clamped
10-40; `mix` = 10 near the target (< 0.5σ) else `sig` (`target-rules.js` `sigW`, `:125`).
