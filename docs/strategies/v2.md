## Intent

The far end of the cover-geometry axis: put the cover's short leg **at the money** rather than at the
covered position's short strike. Widest tent, most terminal potential, dearest cover — and, uniquely
among v0-v3, a geometry that **can push open + cover past the width and forfeit the guaranteed floor**.
`index.js:188-190`: "that is the trade being measured"; `spread-logic.js:181-184` repeats it.

## Open signal

Identical to v0/v1: `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`) — green + new
high → **bull**; red + new low → **bear**. Signal on /NQ, pricing on NDX, strikes by placement G.

## Cover signal

Trigger conditions and order life identical to v0 — see [v0.md](v0.md#cover-signal).

Geometry (`coverShortFor('underlying', …)`, `spread-logic.js:186-193`):

```
coverShort = grid(underlying)
```
clamped so it never moves backward past the position's own short strike. "Underlying" is NDX (the pricing
instrument) at cover-placement time (`trader.js:1423`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

Identical to v0 — single 15m candle direction plus the prior-extreme break. No structural trend.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **The floor guarantee is genuinely optional for this family and nothing v2-specific guards it.** The
   governor's book-floor projection is what catches a cover that breaks it; there is no documented decision
   about whether a per-position guard is wanted.
2. **Geometry expression under G** — as v1 item 1: not re-measured since 2026-09-08.
3. **Backtest reversal covers ignore geometry** (`coverGeometryOnReversal` unset; README #10).
4. **v2-10 is a two-flag cell** (ladder-off control + flies). Results there are suggestive, not
   attributable (`index.js:34-38`, `:500-503`).
