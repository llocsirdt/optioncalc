## Intent

The far end of the cover-geometry axis: put the cover's short leg **at the money** rather than at the
covered position's short strike. Widest tent, most terminal potential, dearest cover — and, uniquely
among v0-v3, a geometry that **can push open + cover past the width and forfeit the guaranteed floor**.
`index.js:189-192`: "that is the trade being measured"; `trader.js:2940-2950` (the `selectCoverGeometric`
comment) repeats it.

## Open signal

Identical to v0/v1: `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`) — green + new
high → **bull**; red + new low → **bear**. Signal on /NQ, pricing on NDX, strikes by placement G.

## Cover signal

Trigger conditions and order life identical to v0 — see [v0.md](v0.md#cover-signal).

Geometry (`coverShortFor('underlying', …)`, `spread-logic.js:186-201`):

```
coverShort = grid(underlying)
```
clamped so it never moves backward past the position's own short strike. "Underlying" is NDX (the pricing
instrument) at cover-placement time. As on v1 it is read by **continuous covers at every width**
(`selectCoverGeometric`) and by **signal covers** (`selectCoverFixed`). From 2026-10-13 to 2026-11-06, 20/40W
continuous covers took their strike from the cover range, which ignores `coverGeometry`; since 2026-11-06 v0–v2 skip
the cover range (`index.js` `COVER_GEOMETRY_BASELINES`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

Identical to v0 — single 15m candle direction plus the prior-extreme break. No structural trend.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **Resolved 2026-11-06: geometry was a 10W-only test** from 2026-10-13 (see [v1.md](v1.md#open-questions--discrepancies) item 1).
2. **The floor guarantee is genuinely optional for this family and nothing v2-specific guards it.** The
   governor's floor check on a cover fill (the mark path at fill time; `coverBreachesGovernor`, `trader.js:926`,
   under the broker) is what catches a cover that breaks it; there is no documented decision about whether a
   per-position guard is wanted.
3. **Backtest reversal covers ignore geometry** (`coverGeometryOnReversal` unset, `backtest-v6-5m.js:1887`).
4. **Stale watchlist:** v2-10 is still on `WATCHLIST` as a "no ladder" control (`index.js:52-55`) though it
   carries the ladder.
