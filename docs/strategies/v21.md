## Intent

[v20](v20.md) with the **strike chosen by price instead of placement G**: the vertical whose real mark is
closest to a fixed fraction of the width. 18 variants: `v21-{10,20,40}-{f50,f55,f60}`, each with a `-unc` twin;
no -cATM.

## Open signal

**v20's signal exactly** (`bb-fade.OPENS_ONLY_SIGNAL`: 5m /NQ close beyond both the last completed 15m and 60m
BB(20,2) → fade). Everything except strike selection and the walk cap is v20's: one working open, open walk
ladder, day-loss cap on opens (none on -unc), leg ledger, 09:30-15:55 window, no covers or hedges of any kind,
held to the 16:00 NDX settle. On 2026-11-04 it fired on the same eight 09:45-10:20 bear candles as v20 and never
again (signal trace: `bb-fade-bear` 144 over the 18 variants).

**Strike — price target** (`price-target.js pickByPrice`, `:29`; live `trader.js buildOpenPriceTarget`, `:2702`):
of every width-W vertical with its short strike within ±12 strikes of the money, take the one whose mark is
closest to **f × W**; on a tie, the one whose centre is nearest spot. No decline: the price *is* the decision.
Live, each candidate is priced on the real chain through `buildOpenAtStrikes` with every quote gate (a refused or
unquoted candidate is skipped); the backtest prices with Black-Scholes.

**Walk cap** (`walkCapFracFor`, `:46`): the open is placed at its mark ceiled to the tick and may walk to
`max(limit, capFrac × W)`, with `capFrac = f + (parent capFrac − 0.50)` (`priceTargetFinish`). Roster values:

| W | f50 | f55 | f60 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 0.55 | 0.6 | 0.65 |
| 20 | 0.575 | 0.625 | 0.675 |
| 40 | 0.5875 | 0.6375 | 0.6875 |

`openWalkCapFrac` stays the parent's (0.55 / 0.575 / 0.5875) on the roster but is unused on this path: the
price-target search prices with `openWalkCapFrac: null` and `priceTargetFinish` sets the cap from `capFrac` alone.
