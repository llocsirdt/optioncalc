# v6 — "5m-harness"

`{ key: 'v6', label: '5m-harness', signalFn: v6Signal, signalCfg: { fiveMin: true }, testAtBase: true, ...PORTED_COVER }`
— `server/src/candle-spread/index.js:179`

Signal: `server/src/candle-spread/signals/v6-signals.js`. Note: **no `at15` wrapper** — v6 is called
on every 5m bar.

## Intent

Fix v5's **lag**, not its logic. v5's flip reads the 15m midline, a ~20-bar (~5 h) average, which
turns far too late on two-sided days (8/14: v5 kept opening bears into the afternoon base long after
price had turned). Four attempts to read a *faster* trend while still **sampling at 15m closes** all
failed — "at a 15m close the snapshot holds only the ONE 5m candle aligned with that close; the other
2 of every 3 are invisible … So no 15m-cadence rule can truly 'watch the 5m'"
(`v6-signals.js:90-95`). v6 is therefore a **harness** change: step the engine at 5m and act between
15m bars, with the signal defaults otherwise identical to v5.

## Open signal

Two paths, chosen by whether this bar is also a 15m close (`ctx.isFifteen`, set by the engine at
`index.js:658` / the backtest's `bars[i].fifteen`).

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v6-signals.js:80-88`) — the new behaviour, gated on `cfg.fiveMin === true`:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| nothing held | no action (`intra-5m hold`) |
| holding **bull**, 5m structural trend is **down** on **this bar and the prior 5m bar** | cover; **and** flip to bear if the 15m structural trend also agrees (`fiveMinFlip`, default on) |
| holding **bear**, 5m trend **up** for two consecutive bars | cover; flip to bull if 15m agrees |
| anything else | no action |

The two-bar requirement is the churn filter: "the second confirms the first, no single-candle churn"
(`v6-signals.js:76-77`).

**(b) 15m closes** — the full v5 cascade, unchanged in its defaults:

| # | Trigger | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover (`fastCover5m`) — **OFF** | `:96-103` |
| 0 | Trend-flip (structural cross against the held book) | `:113-117` |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`reversalFrac −0.15`) | `:128-140` |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`widenFrac 0.10`) | `:146-159` |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** | `:162-172` |
| 4 | Active covering (`activeCoverRadius 8`) | `:178-188` |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`pausePts 8`), with an optional `openGate5m` veto — **OFF** | `:194-206` |

## Cover signal

| Trigger | Condition | Order placed | Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **intra-5m early cover** (new) | 5m structural trend against the held side for 2 consecutive 5m bars | resting, at `W − openCost`, on that 5m bar | when the real mark reaches it |
| trend-flip | 15m close, structural cross against the book | resting, `W − openCost` | same |
| overextension / grind reversal | as v5 | same | same |
| active-cover | held side into a ≥3-TF opposing cluster | same | same |
| `continuous` (shared) | **every 5m bar, on every uncovered position with no cover working — unarmed, so effectively at birth** | `W − openCost − 0.35×W` | same |
| `lock` (shared) | governor needs room for a blocked open | `W − openCost` | same |

All are resting orders. Nothing books at placement; the only immediate-book path
(`lockCoverMode: 'mark'`) is not used — `BASE_RUNS` sets `'rest'` (`index.js:104`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend of a timeframe** = that TF's `close` **and** its 9EMA on the same side of that
  TF's BB midline (`trendOf`, `v6-signals.js:38-41`).
- **Which timeframe** is `cfg.trendTf`, default **`'15m'`** — i.e. v6 reads the trend exactly as v5
  does. `'5m'` and `'both'` exist and are measured to hurt.
- **Direction has changed:**
  - at a 15m close — the 15m structural pair crosses against the held book (as v5);
  - intra-15m — the **5m** structural pair is against the held book for **two consecutive 5m bars**,
    which covers immediately and flips only if 15m agrees.
- Reversal reads (wick rejection at the 5m band, 1m band expansion + candle formation) unchanged from
  v4/v5.

## Lineage

- **Inherits from:** v5, frozen. `v6-signals.js` is `v5-signals.js` plus `trendOf`, the `trendTf`
  knob, the intra-5m branch, `fastCover5m` and `openGate5m`.
- **Changed:** the *cadence*. With `fiveMin` off, v6 is byte-identical to v5 (`v6-signals.js:79`).
- **v6 is the current best signal.** Experiments log: "CURRENT BEST = v6 signal on the 5m engine."

## Findings

Committed baseline (765 days):

| variant | total $ | worst day $ | win % | opens/day | maxDD30 $ | ret/maxDD30 | peak capital $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v6-10 | 1,815,339 | −5,965 | 76% | 24.8 | −17,031 | 106.6 | 1,731 |
| v6-20 | 2,699,959 | −7,000 | 65% | 29.2 | −26,768 | 100.9 | 3,924 |
| v6-40 | 2,822,661 | −9,000 | 56% | 28.4 | −55,299 | 51.0 | 8,158 |

**v6-20 and v6-10 are both on the live watchlist** (set 2026-09-07).

Measured, from the experiments log and the v4→v7 narrative:
- The 5m step is worth **+27% to +37% terminal** on the honest 5m fill model (85 days: floor
  $214.2k/term $143.8k with intra-5m OFF → $239.7k/$183.0k ON). Holds in- and out-of-sample.
- `fiveMinFlip: false` (cover-only intra-5m) is **worse** — +$135k vs +$183k: it exits the trend
  without re-entering and misses the continuation.
- Every faster-trend knob at 15m cadence **hurts**: `trendTf '5m'` $96k vs v5's $156k, `'both'`
  $132k; `openGate5m` helps 8/14 specifically (−$7.7k → −$6.8k) but costs the aggregate;
  `fastCover5m` is worst of all ($105k / $79k) and even lowers the floor.
- `minLockFrac 0.35` for v6 is one of only three values actually **swept** (widths 10/20,
  `index.js:277`).
- `openNeverOtm` on v6-20: **+$21,582** over 765 days. On v6-40: +$4,630.
- The 2026-09-08 **cover ladder sweep** (work the resting limit up toward the market) cost v6-20
  **−$460k at best and −$864k at worst across 36 arms** — including the cap-0% arm that never books a
  loss. Conclusion recorded as settled: forcing cover fills destroys more than the naked positions
  cost. Do not re-open from a single session.
- Remaining tension from the original build: the intra-5m flip drives the aggregate gain but churns
  on the choppiest days (8/14 got *worse*). "Tune intra-5m selectivity (3-bar confirm / require
  band break-and-close-beyond / chop filter on tight 5m bands)" was the stated next step — not built.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **🚨 CONFIRMED DRIFT — "continuous cover" is not what the user meant.**
   As implemented (`backtest-v6-5m.js:560-587`, `trader.js:669-698`) it rests a cover on **every
   uncovered position on every bar**, which for an unarmed family means **the instant the position
   opens**. The user has stated this is **not** the intended meaning: opening a **new** position is
   itself the trigger to cover a **prior** one — a ladder. The corrected reading is written into the
   engine at `backtest-v6-5m.js:855-861` behind `opts.coverPriorOnOpen`, **which no family and no
   variant sets**, and it has **no live implementation** at all — `trader.js:838-840` still carries
   the original `coverTiming: 'each-candle'` placeholder ("cover the prior uncovered position on
   every candle … semantics TBD"), with `'on-reversal'` the only implemented value.
   Consequences measured: 101 of 101 live covers on 2026-09-08 came from `continuous`; the
   trend-flip, active-cover, proactive and cover-to-stack triggers are all starved because they
   filter on `!pendingCover`.
   **Every v6 baseline number above was produced under the unintended rule.**
2. **CONFIRMED — cover limits never consult the market.** `target = W − openCost − minLock`
   (`trader.js:118-131`). Live 2026-09-08 v6-20 covered 14 of 26 (56%); unfilled limits sat at a
   **median 40% of the mark** and were never repriced. Live 2026-09-09, across all variants:
   **733 of 735 unfilled covers were below the market at placement, a median 64% below.** A
   `coverPriceMode: 'mark'` alternative exists **uncommitted** in the working tree and is set by no
   family. Direct conflict with the standing
   principle "we make money on COVERS, not opens — the opens must fill".
3. **`minLock` is applied uniformly.** The user's stated model is that a *reversal* cover may
   rationally lock ~zero or a small loss because the upside lives in the tent's shape, while a
   *winner/risk-relief* cover should take what the market gives. v6 applies 0.35×W to continuous
   covers and 0 to everything else, which is neither. *Documented intent, not implemented.*
4. **Open, stated, not built:** intra-5m selectivity against chop (3-bar confirm / band
   break-and-close / tight-band filter).
5. **Vestigial flag:** `testAtBase: true` is read by nothing (`index.js:175-176`); arming is decided
   solely by the `CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED` env var.
