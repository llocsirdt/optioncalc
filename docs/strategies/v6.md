# v6 — "5m-harness"

`{ key: 'v6', label: '5m-harness', signalFn: v6Signal, signalCfg: { fiveMin: true }, testAtBase: true, ...PORTED_COVER }`
— `server/src/candle-spread/index.js:209`

Signal: `server/src/candle-spread/signals/v6-signals.js` (unchanged since 2026-08-31). **No `at15`
wrapper** — v6 is called on every 5m bar. Shared machinery:
[README.md](README.md#shared-machinery-read-this-before-any-family-file).

## Intent

Fix v5's **lag**, not its logic. v5's flip reads the 15m midline (~5 h average), which turns far too late
on two-sided days. Faster-trend reads sampled at 15m closes all failed — "at a 15m close the snapshot holds
only the ONE 5m candle aligned with that close; the other 2 of every 3 are invisible … So no 15m-cadence
rule can truly 'watch the 5m'" (`v6-signals.js:90-95`). v6 is therefore a **harness** change: step at 5m
and act between 15m bars, with signal defaults otherwise identical to v5.

## Open signal

Two paths, chosen by whether this bar is also a 15m close (`ctx.isFifteen`; live from `markIsFifteen`,
`index.js:1331`; backtest `bars[i].fifteen`).

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v6-signals.js:80-88`), gated on `cfg.fiveMin === true`:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| nothing held | no action |
| holding **bull**, 5m structural trend **down** on this bar **and** the prior 5m bar | cover; flip to bear if the 15m structural trend also agrees (`fiveMinFlip`, default on) |
| holding **bear**, 5m trend **up** for two consecutive bars | cover; flip to bull if 15m agrees |
| anything else | no action |

The two-bar requirement is the churn filter (`v6-signals.js:76-77`).

**(b) 15m closes** — the full v5 cascade, unchanged in its defaults:

| # | Trigger | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover (`fastCover5m`) — **OFF** | `:96-103` |
| 0 | Trend-flip (structural cross against the held book) | `:113-117` |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`reversalFrac −0.15`) | `:128-140` |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`widenFrac 0.10`) | `:146-159` |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** | `:162-172` |
| 4 | Active covering (`activeCoverRadius 8`) | `:178-188` |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`pausePts 8`), optional `openGate5m` veto — **OFF** | `:194-206` |

## Cover signal

Signal covers (intra-5m early cover, trend-flip, overext/grind, active-cover) cover **both sides** and
place break-even resting orders on positions without a cover working. The shared layer (README) runs on
every 5m bar regardless of the signal path: continuous cover at `W − openCost − minLock` from the first
candle after a fill, cover ladder to break-even (not on the v6-10 control cell), give-up at 10 points
through the short strike with a 5% / 7.5% / 10% of width allowance, governor deferral, floor raise, wings,
flies. As in v4/v5, continuous covering usually places the order; the signal's intra-5m cover acts
mainly through the stance reset, the flip open and the cancellation of a working open.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend of a timeframe** = that TF's `close` **and** 9EMA on the same side of its BB midline
  (`trendOf`, `v6-signals.js:38-41`).
- **Which timeframe** is `cfg.trendTf`, default **`'15m'`** (v5's read). `'5m'` and `'both'` measured to hurt.
- **Direction has changed:** at a 15m close, the 15m pair crosses against the book; intra-15m, the 5m pair
  is against the held side for **two consecutive 5m bars** — covers at once, flips only if 15m agrees.

## Findings

| variant | total $ | avg/day $ | worst day $ | win % | maxDD30 $ | ret/maxDD30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v6-10 | 947979 | 1239 | -2055 | 68% | -10828 | 87.5 |
| v6-20 | 1795107 | 2347 | -4985 | 67% | -19280 | 93.1 |
| v6-40 | 2075246 | 2713 | -9311 | 54% | -55575 | 37.3 |

Signal-design findings (85-day honest 5m fill model, 2026-08; held on 762 NQ days):
- The 5m step is worth **+27% to +37% terminal**, in- and out-of-sample.
- `fiveMinFlip: false` (cover-only intra-5m) is **worse** — exits the trend without re-entering.
- Every faster-trend knob at 15m cadence **hurts** (`trendTf '5m'`, `'both'`, `openGate5m`, `fastCover5m`).

Later results that explain today's config:
- **minLock (2026-09-12):** on v6-20 0.20 beat the old 0.35 outright (+12.5 fill points, +$158,885); that
  finding started the fleet move away from per-family locks (`index.js:607-611`).
- **Cap:** v6-20 raised $2,000 → $4,500 on 2026-09-27 (at 4,500 ret/DD 140.7, "the PEAK",
  `index.js:716-717`); v6-40 tuned to $8,500, then held at the $7,500 ceiling (trade: total −$370k for ret/DD
  36.6 → 39.5 and a smaller maxDD, `index.js:540`).
- **Ladder control cell** — see [v1.md](v1.md#findings).

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Signal covers pre-empted by continuous covering** (README #1) — the intra-5m early cover, which is
   v6's defining addition, mostly acts through the flip and the stance rather than by placing a cover.
2. **Open, stated, not built:** intra-5m selectivity against chop (3-bar confirm / band break-and-close /
   tight-band filter). The intra-5m flip drives the aggregate gain but churns on the choppiest days.
3. **Vestigial flag:** `testAtBase: true` is read by nothing; arming is `CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED` alone.
4. **v6-10 is a two-flag cell** (ladder-off control + flies); read results there as suggestive.
