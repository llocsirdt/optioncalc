## Intent

Fix v5's **lag**, not its logic. v5's flip reads the 15m midline (~5 h average), which turns far too late
on two-sided days. Faster-trend reads sampled at 15m closes all failed — "at a 15m close the snapshot holds
only the ONE 5m candle aligned with that close; the other 2 of every 3 are invisible … So no 15m-cadence
rule can truly 'watch the 5m'" (`v6-signals.js:90-95`). v6 is therefore a **harness** change: step at 5m
and act between 15m bars, with signal defaults otherwise identical to v5.

## Open signal

Two paths, chosen by whether this bar is also a 15m close (`ctx.isFifteen`; live from `markIsFifteen`,
`index.js:1675`; backtest `bars[i].fifteen`).

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v6-signals.js:80-88`), gated on `cfg.fiveMin === true`:

| Condition | Result |
|---|---|
| nothing held | no action |
| holding **bull**, 5m structural trend **down** on this bar **and** the prior 5m bar | cover; flip to bear if the 15m structural trend also agrees (`fiveMinFlip`, default on) |
| holding **bear**, 5m trend **up** for two consecutive bars | cover; flip to bull if 15m agrees |
| anything else | no action |

The two-bar requirement is the churn filter (`v6-signals.js:73-79`).

**(b) 15m closes** — the full v5 cascade, unchanged in its defaults:

| # | Trigger | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover (`fastCover5m`) — **OFF** | `:90-103` |
| 0 | Trend-flip (structural cross against the held book) | `:105-117` |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`reversalFrac −0.15`) | `:119-140` |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`widenFrac 0.10`) | `:142-159` |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** | `:161-172` |
| 4 | Active covering (`activeCoverRadius 8`) | `:174-188` |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`pausePts 8`); `htfGate` and `openGate5m` vetoes — **OFF** | `:190-208` |
| 6 | Green-breaks-high (`breakOpen`) — **OFF** | `:210-212` |

**Cover to open, wait** (`coverToOpenWait: true`, every v6 variant since 2026-10-16; `index.js:1076`): an open
signal on a side that is already **underwater** — an uncovered position on it marks below what it paid
(`underwaterOnSide`, `trader.js:3828`) — escalates those positions' covers to the give-up path (`c2o-escalate`)
and the open is skipped (`open-skip-c2o-wait`) until the side is clear (`trader.js:2333-2346`). Opposite-side
opens are unaffected. The backtest models it (`opts-for.js:106-108` → `coverToOpen 'same'` + wait).

## Cover signal

Signal covers (intra-5m early cover, trend-flip, overext/grind, active-cover) cover **both sides** and
place break-even resting tents on positions without a cover working. The shared layer runs on every pass
regardless of the signal path: continuous cover from the first pass after a fill (10W: the plateau cover —
`offset1`, minLock 0.05×W, give-up back to the tent; 20/40W: the cover range, minLock 0.20×W, give-up
`inside`), cover ladder, stall cover at 15 min, give-up at 20 points through the short strike with a 5% / 7.5% /
10% of width allowance, governor deferral, floor raise, wings, floor offsets. Continuous covering usually places
the order; the signal's intra-5m cover acts mainly through the stance reset, the flip open and the cancellation
of a working open.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend of a timeframe** = that TF's `close` **and** 9EMA on the same side of its BB midline
  (`trendOf`, `v6-signals.js:38-41`).
- **Which timeframe** is `cfg.trendTf`, default **`'15m'`** (v5's read; `:63`). `'5m'` and `'both'` measured to
  hurt.
- **Direction has changed:** at a 15m close, the 15m pair crosses against the book; intra-15m, the 5m pair
  is against the held side for **two consecutive 5m bars** — covers at once, flips only if 15m agrees.
- **Underwater** (for cover-to-open-wait) is a book-state read, not a market read: mark < price paid.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **Signal covers pre-empted by continuous covering** — the intra-5m early cover, v6's defining addition,
   mostly acts through the flip and the stance rather than by placing a cover.
2. **Cover-to-open-wait costs opens by design.** Its adoption comment (`trader.js:2333-2338`) records better worst
   day / maxDD / floor on every variant swept at the cost of fewer opens; v9 is the control.
3. **Open, stated, not built:** intra-5m selectivity against chop (3-bar confirm / band break-and-close /
   tight-band filter).
4. **Comment vs code:** `v6-signals.js:60` labels `'5m'` "(fast; DEFAULT)" while the code default is `'15m'`
   (`:63`); the header's "Signals (2)-(4) are OFF" is false for (2)/(4).
5. **Vestigial flag:** `testAtBase: true` is read by nothing; arming is `CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED` / strategy-control.
6. **Stale watchlist:** v6-10 is still on `WATCHLIST` as a "no ladder" control (`index.js:52-55`).
