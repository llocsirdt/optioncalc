## Intent

The [v6](v6.md) strategy with the book protected by **opens instead of per-position covers**: a
reversal-open (REV) on the other side naturally offsets the stack, an ADD gate stops stacking except at the
band, and a jump guard covers a gap. Defined 2026-10-17 (ef8b80c, `data/sweeps/v12-v13-2026-10-17`). Cloned
over **every** finished v6 shape (10/20/40, -unc 10/20/40, -cATM 20/40 = 8); the sweep defined it on v6-10/20/40
only.

## Open signal

The v6 signal (`v6Signal`, `{ fiveMin: true }`, not bidirectional) runs unchanged. On each candle, after the
signal and before the cover set (`trader.js:2093-2109`; an add that fails is recorded `open-skip-natural-add`), `naturalDecide` applies (constants from
`natural-cover.js configOf`, `:36`):

| Rule | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Live positions** | not hedge, not covered, not offset, not a guard. **Bias** = the side with more live positions; tie → the most recent open's side (`biasOf`, `:64`) |
| **REV** (`revCheck`, `:80`) | opens the side opposite the bias when ALL hold: **touch** — the bias side's outer band touched (within 3 pt) on the NDX-RTH 5m band or the /NQ 60m band (`revTouch 'rth5\|nq60'`); **speed** — the last bias-side open is ≤ 30 min old; **run** — the zigzag leg (θ = 20 pt on NDX 1m closes) in the bias direction is ≥ 40 pt, or ≥ 60 pt when no band / 9EMA line (NQ shifted by the basis, or RTH) lies within 12 pt of its extreme; and the candle is not a mid/9EMA-only touch. A REV takes the candle's open and drops the signal's cover |
| **REV strike** (`pickRevStrike`, `:134`, `revStrike 'same'`) | the paired position's **short** strike; if its mark is not in (0, capFrac × W], one strike cheaper, then dearer; else the variant's own placement. Pair + REV must cost ≤ 1.15 × W |
| **REV fills** | marks its pair **offset** (naturally covered) |
| **ADD gate** (`addGate`, `:171`) | a signal open on a side that already holds ≥ 1 live position needs the last completed /NQ 15m candle at its outer band on that side (within 3 pt); at most 3 adds a side |
| **Jump guard** (`guardX` `:183`, `guardPick` `:197`) | X = the 15-min p95 move by time of day (150 / 120 / 85 / 80 / 85 pt for 09:30-10 / 10-11 / 11-14 / 14-15 / 15-16) × IV / `guardMedianIv`. If the book marked at spot ± X is through −`lossMax`, open the protective vertical that maximises the floor over that region (≤ 6 per day) |

A natural REV bypasses the conflict guard and the cover-to-open wait (`trader.js:2348`, `:2382`).

## Cover signal

**None per position.** Signal covers and cover-to-stack are refused and recorded as `cover-skip-natural`
(`trader.js:185-188`, `:2259`, `:2490-2491` — "natural cover never locks a winner to make room"); the proactive
pass is gated off (`:2313`) and the continuous-cover pass returns at once for v12 (`:1892`). (`coverToStack: true` is still on the
roster, but that branch is reached only when `capState` (`:447`) fails on `softCap` / `hardCap` / `riskCap`,
all `null` on every variant — it never fires live on any family.) Backtest: `noPpc` (`backtest-v6-5m.js:359`).
Give-up and stall act on covers, so they have nothing to act on. Hedges (floor raise, wings, floor offsets) and the
governor are v6's.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **The guard is off on -unc.** Its threshold is −`lossMax` (`guardLimit`, `:188`), and -unc has
   `lossMax: null`, so `guardBreach` never fires there.
2. **The roster uses `revStrike 'same'`** (the default), not `'curve'`, the one sweep arm that improved floor
   metrics at every width.
3. **-unc and -cATM were never in the sweep**; their only numbers are the baselines above.
4. `effectiveDryRun` / `buildEngineDeps` comments still name only "v20 / v21" as simulate-only
   (`index.js:1722`, `:2186`); the code applies to v12 / v13 too.
