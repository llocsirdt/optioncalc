# v1 — "classic halfway"

## Intent

Measure the **cover-geometry axis**: instead of pinning the offsetting spread to the position's own short
strike, walk it halfway toward the underlying. Open + cover then form a **condor** — a plateau rather than
a peak — trading some locked floor for a wider region that pays. Signal identical to v0 so the only
variable is where the cover sits (`index.js:187-190`, `spread-logic.js:186-193`).

## Open signal

Identical to v0: `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`, `at15` at
`index.js:93`) — green 15m candle **and** new high → **bull**; red **and** new low → **bear**. Signal on
/NQ, pricing on NDX, strikes by placement G.

## Cover signal

Trigger conditions and order life identical to v0 — see [v0.md](v0.md#cover-signal).

**What differs is the geometry.** `coverShortFor('halfway', …)`, `spread-logic.js:186-193`:

```
coverShort = grid((positionShortStrike + underlying) / 2)
```
clamped so the cover never moves backward past the position's own short strike (bull: `max`, bear:
`min`). Legs via `coverLegsAtShort`. Live, both the continuous path (`selectCoverGeometric`,
`trader.js:2104-2113`) and the reversal path (`selectCoverFixed`, `trader.js:2064-2079`) honour the
geometry. The underlying is NDX at cover-placement time.

## Trend / reversal recognition

Identical to v0 — single 15m candle, close-vs-open direction, prior-extreme break as the trend-intact
test. No structural trend model.

Measured results that still explain the config:
- **Ladder control cell, re-checked 2026-10-04** (give-up on, new ladder pace): ladder on vs off on the
  three control cells is ~flat on avg/day (−$36..+$51), shallower maxDD on 5 of 6, smaller average loss day
  6 of 6 (v1-10 −$755 → −$648), +3-4 fill points. "With give-up on the ladder is a risk/shape tool, not a
  return tool." Recommendation to standardise the ladder ON is pending the user (experiments log).
- **Cap:** v1-20 was normalised to its own `-cATM` twin at $4,500 on 2026-09-30 — its ret/DD curve was
  flat ($3,000-$7,000), so the old $7,000 was arbitrary (`index.js:771-777`).
- **2026-09-08 (historical):** with covers chosen at birth while the underlying sat at the short strike,
  71% of live positions got identical cover legs across v0/v1/v2. That measurement predates placement G.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Does the geometry express now?** Under placement G the short leg is often 1-3 strikes ITM at birth,
   so the halfway strike usually differs from the tent's even when the cover is placed immediately. The
   identical-legs rate has not been re-measured since 2026-09-08.
2. **Backtest reversal covers ignore geometry** unless `coverGeometryOnReversal` (set by nothing,
   `backtest-v6-5m.js:1180-1191`); live reversal covers honour it (README #10). Small while continuous
   covering starves the reversal path.
3. **Intent not documented:** whether "halfway" should be measured from the underlying at cover time or
   at open time. The code uses cover time.
4. **Control-cell overlap:** v1-10 is the one no-experiment cell; whether to keep it (and v2-10/v6-10)
   as ladder controls or standardise the ladder is an open user decision.
