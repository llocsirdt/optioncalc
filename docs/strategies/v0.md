Shared machinery (placement G, open/cover order life, give-up, governor, floor raise …) is in

## Intent

The control. One simple single-timeframe price-action signal and the cheapest, most conservative cover
geometry (a tent that shares the position's short strike, forming a butterfly). v0 exists so v1/v2
(geometry) and v3 (covering policy) can each be read as a one-variable A/B against it, and so the
multi-timeframe lineage (v4-v9) has a naive baseline to beat. `index.js:196-198`: "v0 KEEPS the instant
tent deliberately: it is the control the other three are read against".

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`, `index.js:93` — intra-15m bars return a no-op).

`classicSignal`, `server/src/candle-spread/signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| 15m candle is green (`close >= open`) **and** `high > prior 15m high` | open **bull** (call debit spread) |
| 15m candle is red **and** `low < prior 15m low` | open **bear** (put debit spread) |
| no prior 15m bar | no open |

No Bollinger gate, no confluence gate. Continuation only. The signal is computed on **/NQ**; strikes,
prices and settlement are **NDX**. Strike choice is placement G (README); `v0-*-cATM` uses fixed centred
strikes.

> `spread-logic.classifyOpen` implements the same rule plus a first-candle Bollinger fallback, but it is
> only reached in non-ported mode (`trader.js:1414-1417`); every family supplies a `signalFn`.

## Cover signal

`classicSignal`'s cover condition (`classic-signal.js:12-16`):
- holding **bull**, current 15m candle is red **and did NOT make a new high** → cover
- holding **bear**, current 15m candle is green **and did NOT make a new low** → cover

i.e. the trend "failed to extend". The signal cover covers **all** held positions (classic has no per-side
cover) and resets the stance so the opposite open can proceed (`trader.js:1617-1622`). It also cancels a
still-working open on the covered side.

How covers actually reach the book on v0 (unarmed, so continuous covering dominates):

| Trigger | Placed | Starting price | Then |
|---|---|---|---|
| **continuous** | first candle after the open fills | `W − openCost − minLock` (minLock 0.10×W at 10W, 0.20×W at 20/40W) | cover ladder walks it up to break-even, $0.05 per 120 s / 10 NDX pts; give-up if NDX runs 10 pts through the short strike |
| **reversal** (signal above) | only on positions with no cover working — rare under continuous covering | break-even `W − openCost` | give-up only |
| **cover-to-stack** | not reached live (no legacy cap on v0) | — | — |

Geometry: `coverGeometry: 'tent'` — the cover's short leg sits **at the position's own short strike**
(`selectCoverGeometric` → `selectCoverFixedMark`, `trader.js:2104-2106`). Open + cover form a butterfly
peaked at that strike: cheapest cover, largest locked floor, least terminal upside.

## Trend / reversal recognition

Purely single-bar, single-timeframe:
- **Direction** = the sign of `close − open` on the 15m candle.
- **Trend intact** = the candle also extended the prior bar's high (bull) or low (bear).
- **Reversal** = an opposite-colour candle that failed to extend the prior extreme.

No midline, no EMA, no multi-timeframe context, no structural trend. `classic-signal.js:2-6`: "the simple
baseline the multi-TF lineage (v4-v9) was built to beat; it clusters its worst days on DIFFERENT dates,
so it diversifies."

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Continuous covering pre-empts the classic reversal cover** (README #1). The reversal rule above is
   v0's whole cover *signal*, yet on v0 it mostly governs the stance and open-cancellation rather than
   placing covers.
2. **The `coverSelector` axis is dead** — v0-v3 are all `'fixed'`, and `greedy`/`joint` are reachable
   only from the reversal path (README #9).
3. **Give-up × placement G** (README #3) applies to every family; v0 is the cleanest place to measure it
   because its signal has no structural-trend component.
