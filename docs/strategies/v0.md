## Intent

The control. One simple single-timeframe price-action signal and the cheapest, most conservative cover
geometry (a tent that shares the position's short strike, forming a butterfly). v0 exists so v1/v2
(geometry) and v3 (covering policy) can each be read as a one-variable A/B against it, and so the
multi-timeframe lineage (v4-v9) has a naive baseline to beat. `index.js:193-194`: "v0 KEEPS the instant
tent deliberately: it is the control the other three are read against".

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`, `index.js:95` — intra-15m bars return a no-op).

`classicSignal`, `server/src/candle-spread/signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| 15m candle is green (`close >= open`) **and** `high > prior 15m high` | open **bull** (call debit spread) |
| 15m candle is red **and** `low < prior 15m low` | open **bear** (put debit spread) |
| no prior 15m bar | no open |

No Bollinger gate, no confluence gate. Continuation only. The signal is computed on **/NQ**; strikes,
prices and settlement are **NDX**. Strike choice is placement G (README); `v0-*-cATM` uses fixed centred
strikes.

> `spread-logic.classifyOpen` is only reached when a run has no `signalFn` (`trader.js` `processCandleClose`,
> `ported` at `:1984`); every family supplies one.

## Cover signal

`classicSignal`'s cover condition (`classic-signal.js:12-16`):
- holding **bull**, current 15m candle is red **and did NOT make a new high** → cover
- holding **bear**, current 15m candle is green **and did NOT make a new low** → cover

The signal returns `cover: true` (both sides), resets the stance so the opposite open can proceed, and cancels a
still-working open on the covered side. It places an order only on positions with no cover working; under
continuous covering that is rare (see [signals.md](signals.md)).

What actually covers on v0:

| Trigger | Placed | Price | Strike |
|---|---|---|---|
| **continuous** (unarmed) | every filled position, first pass after the fill | `W − openCost − minLock` (minLock 0.10×W at 10W, 0.20×W at 20/40W), then the cover ladder ($0.05 per 120 s or 10 NDX pts) | 10W: the tent. 20/40W: the **cover range** (`pickCoverK`, 9 strikes inside to 2 beyond the tent, 0.02×W of lock conceded per strike of plateau) |
| **give-up** | NDX 20 pts through the short strike | mark + tick, capped at break-even + 5% / 7.5% / 10% of W | 20/40W re-strikes `inside` first; 10W stays put (`giveUpGeometry` unset) |
| **stall** | 15 min after the fill with no favourable move | break-even, or mark + tick if lower | unchanged |
| **reversal** (signal above) | positions with no cover working | break-even `W − openCost` | `selectCoverFixed` → the tent |
| **cover-to-stack** | never fires live (no `softCap`/`hardCap`/`riskCap`); the backtest locks winners ≥ 0.65×W when the governor blocks an open | — | — |

Geometry: `coverGeometry: 'tent'` — the cover's short leg sits **at the position's own short strike**. Open +
cover form a butterfly peaked at that strike: cheapest cover, largest locked floor, least terminal upside.

## Trend / reversal recognition

Purely single-bar, single-timeframe:
- **Direction** = the sign of `close − open` on the 15m candle.
- **Trend intact** = the candle also extended the prior bar's high (bull) or low (bear).
- **Reversal** = an opposite-colour candle that failed to extend the prior extreme.

No midline, no EMA, no multi-timeframe context. `classic-signal.js:2-5`: "the simple baseline the multi-TF
lineage (v4-v9) was built to beat; it clusters its worst days on DIFFERENT dates, so it diversifies."

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **The geometry axis does not exist at 20/40W.** At those widths the continuous cover's strike comes from the
   cover range (`placeContinuousCovers` → `pickCoverK`, `trader.js:1888-1958`), which ignores `coverGeometry`;
   only signal covers use `selectCoverFixed` (geometry-aware), and the backtest's reversal covers use the tent
   unless `coverGeometryOnReversal` (set by nothing, `backtest-v6-5m.js:1887`). Result: v0/v1/v2 at 20W and 40W
   produce the same baseline. v0's tent survives only at 10W.
2. **Continuous covering pre-empts the classic reversal cover.** The reversal rule is v0's whole cover *signal*,
   yet it mostly governs the stance and open-cancellation rather than placing covers.
3. **The `coverSelector` axis is dead** — v0-v3 are `'fixed'`, v4-v9 `'fixed-mark'`; `greedy`/`joint` are set by
   no family.
4. **Stale comment:** the `FAMILIES` header (`index.js:161-165`) says v0-v3 vary "the cover SELECTOR (fixed / greedy
   / joint / fixed-mark)" and that "caps here are the $20 values; buildVariants scales them by width" — neither is
   true of the code.
