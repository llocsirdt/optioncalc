## Intent

A pure **mean-reversion fade of a two-timeframe Bollinger overshoot**, with **no** v9/v7 machinery after the
open (user, 2026-10-21): every open is held to the 16:00 NDX settlement. 10 variants: 10/20/40 short-ATM and
20/40-cATM, each with a `-unc` twin (no `v20-10-cATM`: there is no v9-10-cATM).

## Open signal

On **every 5m /NQ close** (`bbFadeSide`):

| Condition | Opens |
|---|---|
| 5m close **below both** the 15m lower BB(20,2) **and** the 60m lower BB(20,2) | **bull** |
| 5m close **above both** the 15m upper **and** the 60m upper | **bear** |

The 15m and 60m bands are the **last COMPLETED** candle of that timeframe at the 5m mark (no developing bar):
at 10:05 the 15m band is the 09:45-10:00 bar's. Bidirectional (no stance conflict guard). Signal on /NQ, pricing
and action on cash NDX inside the 09:30-15:55 ET action window.

**Strikes:** v9's placement G on 10/20/40 (deepest-ITM inside `[minDebitFrac 0.45, capFrac] × W`, max 3 ITM /
1 OTM strikes, never fully OTM, `spreadShift` 5/10/20); fixed ATM-centred on -cATM.

**Observed 2026-11-04:** the signal fired only on the eight 5m candles 09:45-10:20 (all bear) on every v20/v21 run
and never again that day (closest miss 10:25, 55 pts inside the bands) — which is why they stopped trading. The
signal-trace counts agree: `bb-fade-bear` 80 over the 10 v20 variants, `bb-fade-bull` 0.

## Cover signal

**None.** `bbFadeSignal(null)` has no parent, so no signal cover. The roster whitelist (`ROSTER_KEEP`,
`opens-only.js:64`) leaves every cover / hedge flag absent, and `ROSTER_OFF` (`:77`) switches the BASE_RUNS
defaults off: `continuousCover: false`, `coverToStack: false` (with `coverToStackMinFrac: null`),
`capitalRecapture: false`, `lossTarget: null`, `floorOffset: false`, `creditCoverFrac: null`,
`openAlternateEvery: null`. So: no continuous / signal / proactive covers, no give-up, ladder, stall, c2o,
cover-to-stack, no floor raise, wings, offsets or flies, no late-floor or ratchet gate.

## What is kept

- **Day-loss cap on opens**: the governor's `lossMax` on the projected book floor (2,000 / 4,500 / 7,500;
  -cATM 6,500 / 7,500), refusing an open or a walk step that would breach it. **None on -unc** (`lossMax`,
  `softCap`, `hardCap`, `riskCap` all null).
- **Open walk ladder**: the parent's effective walk made explicit (`openLadder: true`, `opens-only.js:89`);
  one working open at a time — checked **first**, as `open-skip-pending`, before any cap (`trader.js:2364-2376`);
  cancelled on an opposite signal; re-struck after `openRestrikeMin` 10 at its cap.
- **Leg-uniqueness ledger**, open fallback until 15:55, `openNeverOtm`.
- **Simulated fill realism** (live mark path): `simOpenFillMinLooks: 2` (two-look rule), `simFillAtLimit`. The
  quoted-width gate (`simMaxQuoteWidthFrac`) was removed 2026-11-04 (a6bfb3d): simulated fills judge the mark,
  with the two-look rule and the parity / monotonic / cheap-outlier gates.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **The signal clusters and then goes silent.** Requiring a close beyond *both* completed bands fires in
   bursts (11/04: eight consecutive candles in 35 minutes, then nothing); one-working-open absorbs much of a burst
   (the 10/21 run measured ~40%).
2. **Why keep it rostered** when every variant loses: the stated purpose is observation (does live act on the
   intended signal), not P&L. No exit criterion is recorded.
3. `effectiveDryRun` / `fillSourceFor` comments name only "v20 / v21" as simulate-only; v12 / v13 are too.
