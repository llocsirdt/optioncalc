**v7-10 is the ARMED variant** (`ARMED_VARIANT` default, `index.js:335`; the strategy-control page can move the
armed slot, `armedVariant()`). It runs in **TEST mode** today — `CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED_MODE` defaults to `'test'`
(`index.js:500`): real orders at an intentionally unfillable price, auto-cancelled, with fills simulated off the
mark. Real fillable orders additionally need `CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED_MODE=live`, `CANDLE_SPREAD_LIVE=true` and prod
(README "Arming").

## Intent

Allow the strategy to be **wrong without being flat**. When an opposite signal fires while a position is
underwater, v6 must cover and then re-enter; v7 opens the opposite side **early, without force-covering
the loser** — a hedge rather than a force-close (`v7-signals.js:113-118`; the user's framing:
*"BE WRONG = bidirectional early opposite opens"*). This needs **per-side** state: v7 tracks `heldBull` /
`heldBear` and returns `coverSide` ('bull' | 'bear' | 'both' | null) instead of a single `cover` boolean
(`v7-signals.js:59-63`).

## Open signal

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v7-signals.js:83-93`) — v6's rule, per side: a 2-bar-confirmed 5m structural
reversal against a held side covers *that side*, and flips only if the 15m structural trend agrees.

**(b) 15m closes** — the v6 cascade with **one trigger inserted at 0b**:

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover — **OFF** (`:95-103`) | | |
| 0 | **Trend-flip, per side** (`:105-111`) | held side against a confirmed opposite structural trend | open the opposite side, `coverSide` = **only the wrong-way side** |
| **0b** | **BE WRONG** (`:113-157`) | holding a side **and** a reversal candle **and** the candle breaks the prior 15m extreme against the held side | open the opposite side, **`coverSide: null`** — the underwater side is NOT covered (`beWrongCover` off) |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`:159-171`) | as v6 | open reversal side; cover the opposite side only if held |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`:173-187`) | as v6 | same |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** (`:189-200`) | | |
| 4 | Active covering, per side (`:202-213`) | held side into a ≥3-TF opposing cluster | cover just that side |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`:215-231`) | structural trend + matching colour, **and not holding the opposite side** | open with the trend |
| 6 | Green-breaks-high (`:233-236`) — **OFF** | | |

Optional be-wrong knobs, **all default OFF**: `beWrongStrong` (close beyond the 5m centerline), `beWrongDirMin`
(rolling-directionality chop filter), `centerlineCross` (midline close as an extra trigger), `beWrongCover`
(`'reversal'|'always'`: also cover the wrong-way side).

Engine side, `bidirectional: true` bypasses the conflict guard that otherwise refuses an open opposite the held
stance (`trader.js:2382`, `open-skip-conflict`; backtest `backtest-v6-5m.js:196`). An opposite-side signal still
cancels a working open on the other side.

**Cover to open, wait** (`coverToOpenWait`, every v7 variant since 2026-10-16): an open on a side that is
already underwater escalates that side's covers to give-up and waits — see [v6.md](v6.md#open-signal). It reads
only positions on the side being opened, so a be-wrong open (onto the side not held) is not held back by the
underwater side it hedges.

**Reopen after a quick cover** (`reopenOnCover`, every v7 variant since 2026-11-06, fleet-wide v0–v9 in `index.js`
`applyExperiments`; v7-10 only from 2026-10-14, user: "the user trades it this way by hand"): `reopenAfterCover`
(`trader.js:3235-3306`) re-opens the same side after a cover **fills**, when the position was opened for the
current 15m signal and that signal has not since covered the side, and NDX is still inside the original strikes. Same strikes, or deeper ITM while the mark
stays ≤ the price paid; limit capped at the price paid; at most 2 per 5m bar; every open gate applies (governor,
late floor, ratchet, cap, leg uniqueness without a shift, and cover-to-open-wait). An unfilled reopen gives way at
the next 15m close. Modelled in the backtest since 2026-11-06 (`backtest-v6-5m.js`): it runs at the close of the bar
the cover filled in and works from the next bar, so at most one bar later than live.

## Cover signal

| Trigger | v7 behaviour |
|---|---|
| **be-wrong** | places **no cover** — that is the point |
| trend-flip / overext / grind / active-cover / intra-5m | covers **only the targeted side** with a break-even resting tent, on positions with no cover working |
| shared layer (README) | continuous cover — 10W plateau (`offset1`, minLock 0.05×W, give-up back to the tent), 20/40W cover range (minLock 0.20×W, give-up `inside`); ladder $0.05 per 120 s / 10 pts; stall cover 15 min; give-up at 20 pts through the short strike, allowance 5% / 7.5% / 10% of W; governor deferral; floor raise; late-day floor 15:00 |

The stance reset is narrower than v5/v6: `st.direction` is cleared only when the cover is `both` or targets the
currently held side (`trader.js:2302-2306`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend** — identical to v6 (`trendOf`, `trendTf` default `'15m'`).
- **Per-side held state** — both sides can be held at once; "against us" is a per-side question.
- **"Wrong" is recognised earlier than "flipped"**: be-wrong fires on a reversal candle plus a break of
  the prior extreme (one bar); the trend-flip needs the slow structural pair to cross.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **Give-up × placement G.** v7-10's G band can place the short leg one strike OTM, where the give-up
   distance is partly used up at birth. Not separately measured at the current 20-point trigger.
2. **be-wrong's stacking shape is unbounded except by the governor.** The ideas floated to bound it —
   faster covering of the underwater side (now partly `beWrongCover`, default off), or capping the
   *minority-side* exposure — were not adopted.
3. **Resolved 2026-11-06: reopenOnCover was a live-only feature on the armed variant.** It is now on every
   v0–v9 variant and modelled in the backtest; the baselines above predate that.
4. **v7 and v9 historically near-identical** (experiments log, 2026-09-30) — two top-ranked families that do not
   diversify each other. v9 now differs by proactive cover and the *absence* of
   cover-to-open-wait; reopen is on both since 2026-11-06 (see [v9.md](v9.md)).
5. **Intent not documented:** why be-wrong requires *both* a reversal candle and a prior-extreme break. The
   filters added on top were measured and rejected; the base conjunction itself was never A/B'd.
6. **Stale comments:** the `v7-signals.js` header (`:2-32`) is v6's docblock verbatim ("v6 = v5 + a FASTER trend
   read") and never describes be-wrong; the `ARMED` comment (`index.js:317`) says the default is `'v6-20'`
   while the code defaults to `v7-10`; the `TUNED_CAPS` comment above `['v7-10', 2000]` (`index.js:788-790`) still
   reads "RAISED … $1,500 -> $1,750", and the cap-precedence comment says it "moves v7-10 … to its measured peak
   $1,500" (`index.js:1087-1089`); the continuous-cover comment says the minLock ramp "ships ON for v7-10"
   (`trader.js:1906-1908`) — no variant sets `minLockRamp`.
