# v7 — "be-wrong"

— `server/src/candle-spread/index.js:210`

Signal: `server/src/candle-spread/signals/v7-signals.js` (unchanged since 2026-08-31). No `at15` wrapper —
acts every 5m. Shared machinery: [README.md](README.md#shared-machinery-read-this-before-any-family-file).

**v7-10 is the ARMED real-money variant** (`ARMED_VARIANT` default, `index.js:310`; sending also needs
`CANDLE_SPREAD_ARMED_MODE=live`, `CANDLE_SPREAD_LIVE=true` and prod — see README "Arming").

## Intent

Allow the strategy to be **wrong without being flat**. When an opposite signal fires while a position is
underwater, v6 must cover and then re-enter; v7 opens the opposite side **early, without force-covering
the loser** — a hedge rather than a force-close (`v7-signals.js:113-118`; the user's framing:
*"BE WRONG = bidirectional early opposite opens"*). This needs **per-side** state: v7 tracks `heldBull` /
`heldBear` and returns `coverSide` ('bull' | 'bear' | 'both' | null) instead of a single `cover` boolean
(`v7-signals.js:59-63`).

## Open signal

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v7-signals.js:86-93`) — v6's rule, per side: a 2-bar-confirmed 5m structural
reversal against a held side covers *that side*, and flips only if the 15m structural trend agrees.

**(b) 15m closes** — the v6 cascade with **one trigger inserted at 0b**:

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover — **OFF** | | `:95-103` |
| 0 | **Trend-flip, per side** (`:108-111`) | held side against a confirmed opposite structural trend | open the opposite side, `coverSide` = **only the wrong-way side** |
| **0b** | **BE WRONG** (`:127-139`) | holding a side **and** a reversal candle **and** the candle breaks the prior 15m extreme against the held side | open the opposite side, **`coverSide: null`** — the underwater side is NOT covered |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`:141-153`) | as v6 | open reversal side; cover the opposite side only if held |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`:156-169`) | as v6 | same |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** (`:172-183`) | | |
| 4 | Active covering, per side (`:185-195`) | held side into a ≥3-TF opposing cluster | cover just that side |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`:197-211`) | structural trend + matching colour, **and not holding the opposite side** | open with the trend |

Optional be-wrong filters, **all default OFF**: `beWrongStrong` (close beyond the 5m centerline),
`beWrongDirMin` (rolling-directionality chop filter), `centerlineCross` (midline close as an extra
trigger, `:130-138`).

Engine side, `bidirectional: true` bypasses the conflict guard that otherwise blocks opening a side
opposite the held stance (`trader.js:1647`, `:1657-1658`; backtest `backtest-v6-5m.js:146`). The engine
still allows **one working open at a time**, so a be-wrong open waits if an open is already resting.

## Cover signal

| Trigger | v7 behaviour |
|---|---|
| **be-wrong** | places **no cover** — that is the point |
| trend-flip / overext / grind / active-cover / intra-5m | covers **only the targeted side** with a break-even resting order, on positions with no cover working |
| shared layer (README) | continuous cover at `W − openCost − minLock` (0.10×W on v7-10, 0.20×W at 20/40) from the first candle after a fill; ladder $0.05 per 120 s / 10 pts up to break-even; give-up at 10 pts through the short strike, loss allowance 5% / 7.5% / 10% of W; governor cover deferral; floor raise |

The stance reset is narrower than v5/v6: `st.direction` is cleared only when the cover is `both` or
targets the currently held side (`trader.js:1620`, backtest `backtest-v6-5m.js:1197`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend** — identical to v6 (`trendOf`, `trendTf` default `'15m'`).
- **Per-side held state** — both sides can be held at once; "against us" is a per-side question.
- **"Wrong" is recognised earlier than "flipped"**: be-wrong fires on a reversal candle plus a break of
  the prior extreme (one bar); the trend-flip needs the slow structural pair to cross.

Signal-design findings:
- **be-wrong's chop tax is not separable from its trend gains.** `beWrongDirMin` swept across dirMin
  0.25-0.65 × window 12-48 bars: chop bleed unchanged, worst day identical in every case. Real-time chop
  detection lags. Recorded conclusion: be-wrong is "effectively a high-variance 'the day is trending' bet".
- `beWrongStrong` didn't help; `centerlineCross` **hurt at every cap** (answering "which centerline":
  neither).

How v7-10's current config was arrived at (each measured over 765 days on the engine of its day):
- **minLock 0.10 + cover ladder (2026-09-12):** 89.1% cover fill vs 84.9% at 0.20 + ladder and 74.5% for
  the no-ladder control, with more total; worst day unchanged (`index.js:372-376`).
- **Cap $1,500 (2026-09-23/27):** the ret/DD peak of the loss-cap sweep; moved off the CAPPRES preset's
  $1,000 (`index.js:879-881`).
- **Give-up fleet-wide (2026-10-04):** v7-10 ret/DD 526 → 979, maxDD −$3,943 → −$2,236.
- **Flies stay off:** with flies v7-10 ret/DD 979 → 814 (2026-10-04 fly re-sweep).
- **Placement G (2026-10-05):** v7-10 avg/day 1,151 → 1,113 with the worst day back inside the cap
  (−1,455 vs −1,624) and opens ~$0.40 cheaper (`index.js:267-269`). **Re-strike 10 min:** v7-10
  1,113 → 1,190 (`index.js:818-820`).
- **Floor raise objective (2026-10-06):** on 10-wides the lowest-point rule locked more days than
  spreads-first (v7-10 55.4% vs 47.9% locked-profit days); the user chose spreads-first for every strategy
  anyway (`index.js:838-843`).
- **Governor design B (2026-10-02):** born from a live v7-10 day — a bear cover filled with three naked
  bulls on and took the floor −1,278 → −1,748 against a $1,500 cap (`trader.js:771-774`).

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Open-ladder raises are not governor-checked** (README #2) — on the real-money variant. Found
   2026-10-04 with a v7-10 ladder-sweep day at −$1,504 against $1,500; the 10-wide walk ($5.30 → $5.50
   cap) keeps the excess small but not zero.
2. **Give-up × placement G** (README #3). v7-10's G band can place the short leg one strike OTM, where
   the 10-point give-up test can be met at birth.
3. **v7-40's cap is held at the floor, not at its measured optimum.** Tuned $3,000 (from a broken-control
   sweep) is lifted to the $6,000 floor; the corrected sweep prefers $8,000, which the $7,500 ceiling would
   cap. `index.js:887-888`: "still looser-than-floor by measurement and wants a separate decision."
4. **be-wrong's stacking shape is unbounded except by the governor.** The ideas floated to bound it —
   faster covering of the underwater side, or capping the *minority-side* exposure (binds on chop, free on
   trend days) — were never built.
5. **v7 and v9 are nearly the same strategy in practice** (+0.99 daily correlation, 2026-09-30) — two
   top-ranked families that do not diversify each other.
6. **Intent not documented:** why be-wrong requires *both* a reversal candle and a prior-extreme break. The
   filters added on top were measured and rejected; the base conjunction itself was never A/B'd.
7. **Doc drift (memory):** the lineage table still describes v7 as "v6 + risk cap, be-wrong SHELVED".
