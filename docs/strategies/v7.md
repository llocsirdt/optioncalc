# v7 — "be-wrong"

`{ key: 'v7', label: 'be-wrong', signalFn: v7Signal, signalCfg: { fiveMin: true, beWrong: true }, bidirectional: true, ...PORTED_COVER }`
— `server/src/candle-spread/index.js:180`

Signal: `server/src/candle-spread/signals/v7-signals.js`. No `at15` wrapper — acts every 5m.

## Intent

Allow the strategy to be **wrong without being flat**. When an opposite signal fires while a position
is underwater, v6 must first cover (realise the state) and then re-enter. v7 instead opens the
opposite side **early, without force-covering the loser** — a hedge rather than a force-close. The
underwater side keeps its tent and its upside; the new side takes the turn.
`v7-signals.js:113-118`; the user's framing in `project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy` is
*"BE WRONG = bidirectional early opposite opens"*.

This required a **per-side** refactor: v7 tracks `heldBull` / `heldBear` separately and returns
`coverSide` ('bull' | 'bear' | 'both' | null) instead of a single `cover` boolean
(`v7-signals.js:59-63`).

## Open signal

Two paths, as v6.

**(a) Intra-15m bars** (`v7-signals.js:86-93`) — identical rule to v6, but per side: a 2-bar-confirmed
5m structural reversal against a held side covers *that side*, and flips only if the 15m structural
trend agrees.

**(b) 15m closes** — the v6 cascade with **one new trigger inserted at position 0b**:

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0a | Fast 5m cover — **OFF** | | `:95-103` |
| 0 | **Trend-flip, per side** (`:108-111`) | held side against a confirmed opposite structural trend | open the opposite side, `coverSide` = **only the wrong-way side** |
| **0b** | **BE WRONG** (`:127-139`) | holding a side **and** the opposite reversal is confirmed: a reversal candle **and** the candle breaks the prior 15m extreme against the held side | open the opposite side, **`coverSide: null`** — the underwater side is NOT covered |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`:141-153`) | as v6 | open reversal side; cover the opposite side only if held |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`:156-169`) | as v6 | same |
| 3 | Candle at confluence — **OFF** (`:172-183`) | | |
| 4 | Active covering, per side (`:185-195`) | held side into a ≥3-TF opposing cluster | cover just that side |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`:197-211`) | structural trend + matching colour, **and not holding the opposite side** | open with the trend |

Two optional be-wrong chop filters exist and **both default OFF**:
`beWrongStrong` (require the close to break the 5m centerline) and `beWrongDirMin` (suppress when
rolling directionality is below a threshold). A third optional trigger, `centerlineCross`
(`:130-138`), adds "price closed across the 5m/15m BB midline against the held side" as a be-wrong
condition — also **OFF**.

Engine-side, `bidirectional: true` bypasses the conflict guard that otherwise blocks opening a side
opposite the held stance (`trader.js:767-771`, `backtest-v6-5m.js:753`).

## Cover signal

Same trigger set as v6 with two behavioural differences:

| Trigger | v7 behaviour |
|---|---|
| **be-wrong** | places **no cover at all** — that is the point |
| trend-flip | covers **only the wrong-way side** (`coverSide: 'bull'` or `'bear'`), leaving right-way positions working |
| overextension / grind / active-cover | per side, same as above |
| `continuous` (shared) | every 5m bar, unarmed → at birth, target `W − openCost − 0.20×W` (**the lowest minLock in the roster**) |
| `lock` (shared) | governor cover-to-continue, `W − openCost` |

The stance reset is narrower than v5/v6: `st.dir` is cleared only when the cover is `both`, or when
it targets the currently-held side (`backtest-v6-5m.js:750`, `trader.js:739-741`) — a partial cover of
the *other* side leaves the stance intact.

All covers remain resting orders placed on the trigger bar and filled on a later bar when the real
mark reaches the limit.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend** — identical to v6 (`trendOf`, `trendTf` default `'15m'`).
- **Per-side held state** — `heldBull` / `heldBear`, so "the trend has turned against us" is now a
  per-side question and both sides can be held at once.
- **"Wrong" is recognised earlier than "flipped"**: be-wrong fires on a *reversal candle plus a break
  of the prior extreme* — a one-bar read — whereas the trend-flip needs the slow structural pair to
  cross. So v7 takes the opposite side before it is willing to declare the trend changed.
- **Centerline cross** (`crossedUp` / `crossedDown`, `:45-52`) implements the user's "crossed a key
  band and closed on the other side" read, but is off by default (measured to hurt).

## Distinguishing config

| Flag | v7 | v6 |
|---|---|---|
| `signalCfg.beWrong` | **`true`** | absent |
| `bidirectional` | **`true`** (engine-level) | absent |
| `signalCfg.fiveMin` | `true` | `true` |
| `cfg.centerlineCross` | off | n/a |
| `cfg.beWrongStrong` / `beWrongDirMin` | off | n/a |
| `continuousCoverMinLockFrac` | **0.20** at every width | 0.35 |
| `proactiveCoverFrac` | unset | unset |

`minLock 0.20` is deliberate: "BIDIRECTIONAL families want lower than the rest … a demanding lock
only suppresses fills they need" (`index.js:274-276`).

## Lineage

- **Inherits from:** v6 (signal cascade, cadence, all defaults).
- **Changed:** the be-wrong trigger, per-side cover state, and the `bidirectional` engine flag.
- **Reversal of an earlier verdict.** The v4→v7 narrative concluded *"v7 = v6 + RISK CAP
  (be-patient) ONLY … both bidirectional be-wrong AND centerline are SHELVED"* — be-wrong "didn't
  earn its complexity" at the caps then in use. The v7 family slot was later redefined as *be-wrong*
  (2026-09-03 run-set rebuild), and the experiments log records the user wanting be-wrong kept
  tracked as "the high-ceiling DEVELOPMENT TARGET". The old risk-cap regime that motivated the
  shelving no longer exists — it was replaced by the day-loss governor on 2026-09-04.

Other measured results:
- **The governor is demonstrably load-bearing on v7-10:** uncapped it makes $3,045,845 with a
  −$11,120 worst day, so the cap nearly halves the worst day for 8% of the total.
- **`-unc` twins show the real ceiling:** v7-40-unc $7,154,612 and v7-20-unc $5,169,736 — by far the
  largest capped→uncapped gaps in the roster. v7 is the family that scales as risk tolerance rises.
- **be-wrong's chop tax is not separable from its trend gains.** `beWrongDirMin` swept across
  dirMin 0.25-0.65 × window 12-48 bars: chop bleed stayed −$207k to −$215k, **worst day identical
  (−$30,460) in every case**, terminal only fell. Real-time chop detection is fundamentally lagging.
  Recorded conclusion: be-wrong is "effectively a high-variance 'the day is trending' bet".
- `beWrongStrong` didn't help ($262k → $222k). `centerlineCross` **hurt at every cap**, on all four
  settings — this answered the user's "which centerline" question: neither.
- `openNeverOtm`: **+$13,326** on v7-10, **+$24,418** on v7-20, −$64 on v7-40.
- Wing conversion on v7-10 is noise (+0.2%, halves disagree in sign) — kept on anyway, because the
  `-unc`/`-cATM` twins must match their siblings for the subtraction to mean anything
  (`index.js:207-217`).
- Live 2026-09-08: v7-10 covered 9 of 19 (47%), v7-20 4 of 12 (33%), v7-40 1 of 5 (**20%**).

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **🚨 CONFIRMED DRIFT — "continuous cover" is not what the user meant.** Identical to v6: the
   implementation rests a cover on **every uncovered position on every bar**
   (`backtest-v6-5m.js:560-587`, `trader.js:669-698`), i.e. at birth for an unarmed family. The user's
   stated intent is a **ladder** — a new open triggers covering a prior position. The ladder exists
   only as the unwired `opts.coverPriorOnOpen` backtest flag (`backtest-v6-5m.js:855-861`) and has no
   live implementation; `trader.js:838-840` is still a commented-out placeholder. All v7 numbers were
   produced under the unintended rule.
   **v7 is the family this matters most for**, because be-wrong deliberately leaves the underwater
   side naked while the new side goes on — precisely the situation the ladder was described to
   handle, and precisely the situation instant covering removes by covering everything at birth.
2. **CONFIRMED — the roster and the narrative disagree about what v7 IS.** `FAMILIES` says be-wrong
   bidirectional; `project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy` and the experiments-log lineage table say "v6
   + risk cap only, be-wrong SHELVED". Anyone reading the memory first will form the wrong model of
   the armed default.
3. **CONFIRMED — cover limits never consult the market**, and v7 has the *worst* live fill rates in
   the watchlist (20-47%). Same root cause as v6 item 2; `coverPriceMode: 'mark'` is an uncommitted,
   unwired fix.
4. **Open risk question, unresolved:** be-wrong's stacking. The per-side refactor made v7 *more*
   aggressive (trend-flip covers only the wrong-way side, be-wrong holds both) — uncapped it reached
   47 opens/day, busiest 74. The governor now bounds the loss, but the ideas floated to bound the
   *shape* — "faster covering of the opposite/underwater side, or cap the MINORITY-side exposure
   specifically (trend day = minority small → free; chop = both sides grow → binds)" — were never
   built.
5. **Intent not documented:** why be-wrong requires *both* a reversal candle and a prior-extreme
   break, rather than either. The two chop filters that were added on top were both measured and
   rejected, but the base conjunction itself was never A/B'd.
