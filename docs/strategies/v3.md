# v3 — "classic tent, risk-armed"

`{ key: 'v3', label: 'classic tent, risk-armed', signalFn: at15(classicSignal), signalCfg: {}, coverSelector: 'fixed', coverFillModel: 'resting', ...RISK_ARMED, coverGeometry: 'tent' }`
— `server/src/candle-spread/index.js:172`, with
`RISK_ARMED = { continuousCoverArmFrac: 0.20, continuousCoverOppRatio: 1.0 }` at `index.js:144`.

## Intent

Measure the **covering-POLICY axis**, with geometry held at `tent` so v0-vs-v3 is a clean A/B: should
a cover be rested the instant a position opens (v0), or only once the position or the book has
*earned* one? The stated case for arming (`index.js:152-156`) is the failure mode instant covering
cannot see — "a couple of deep winners sitting through a reversal while total book risk stays low"
— which `lockDeepWinners` cannot catch either, being gated on the floor *already* breaching
`lossTarget`.

v3 is also, per the roadmap, **"the fix … already built and switched off"** for the geometry collapse
described in [README.md](README.md#continuous-cover--the-confirmed-misinterpretation): it is the only
family where continuous covering does not fire at birth.

## Open signal

Identical to v0-v2. `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`):
green + new high → **bull**; red + new low → **bear**.

## Cover signal

Same three triggers as v0, but the **continuous** trigger is gated. Per bar, per uncovered position
with no cover working, a continuous cover is placed only if **either** arm is satisfied
(`backtest-v6-5m.js:553-587`, `trader.js:679-698`):

| Arm | Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) book risk** | `−bookFloorNow ≥ 0.20 × lossTarget` (i.e. the day's worst case has run to $1,000 of the $5,000 target) | "start covering as we approach the cap" — the governor's own measure |
| **(b) opportunity** | `lockedProfit ≥ 1.0 × coverCost`, where `lockedProfit = W − openCost − coverMark` | this cover stands on its own as a trade regardless of book risk |

If neither arm fires, **no cover order is placed at all** on that bar; the position stays naked and
is re-checked next bar. Live, a cover placed via arm (b) is logged as
`action: 'cover-arm-opportunity'` (`trader.js:691-692`).

Once placed, the resting target and fill model are identical to v0
(`W − openCost − 0.25×W`, filled when the real mark reaches it).

The **reversal** and **lock** triggers are unchanged — and, because arming leaves positions without a
pending cover, they can actually fire on v3, unlike v0-v2.

## Trend / reversal recognition

Identical to v0 — single 15m candle direction plus the prior-extreme break.

## Distinguishing config

| Flag | v3 | v0 |
|---|---|---|
| `continuousCoverArmFrac` | **0.20** | unset (always armed) |
| `continuousCoverOppRatio` | **1.0** | unset |
| `coverGeometry` | `'tent'` (same) | `'tent'` |

`continuousCoverMinLockFrac` 0.25 (`index.js:282`).

**Why 0.20 / 1.0:** measured, not guessed. The 70-combination arming sweep (2026-09-06,
`index.js:138-143`) found (i) the OPPORTUNITY trigger does nearly all the work — at `armFrac 0.8`,
where the risk arm barely fires, adding `oppRatio 1.0` took $606,735 → $813,960 and ret/DD 61.2 →
135.3; and (ii) a *lower* `oppRatio` is uniformly better (1.0 > 1.5 > 2.0 > 3.0), "because the cheap
covers are the ones that pay". The first pass (0.60 / 2.0) sat near the bottom of the grid.

## Lineage

- **Inherits from:** v0 exactly, including `coverGeometry: 'tent'`.
- **Changed:** the covering *policy* only (`RISK_ARMED`).
- **History:** the first (2026-09-05) arrangement put all three geometries behind risk-arming, so the
  geometry comparison ran with a handicap on every arm — and the arming config used was then
  measured to be one of the worst in the grid. Re-slotted 2026-09-06 so v0/v1/v2 vary geometry under
  instant covering and v3 alone carries arming, at its best measured setting (`index.js:163-168`).

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Open, high value: the arming sweep needs re-running.** Per the roadmap, arming ON across v0-v3
   should now be measured for (i) do the geometries finally diverge — expect the identical-legs rate
   well below 71%; (ii) do reversal and cover-to-stack covers start firing at all; (iii) P&L and
   efficiency vs the instant-cover control. Not yet done.
2. **v3 is not the ladder.** Arming *delays* the cover; the user's stated intent is that the **next
   open** triggers the cover of a **prior** position. Those are different rules with different
   cadences, and arming was not proposed as an implementation of the ladder. Do not treat v3 as
   having resolved README drift #1.
3. **Intent not documented:** why the *risk* arm and the *opportunity* arm are OR'd rather than
   staged (e.g. opportunity-only early in the day, risk-armed late). The sweep measured the OR
   combination only.
4. **Silent no-cover trap, all families but visible here:** `if (W − openCost − minLock ≤ 0) continue`
   (`trader.js:684`) places **no order at all** when the open cost is high. At $20 with minLock $5,
   an open at 75% of width produces no cover. Opens ran at a median 53% of width on 2026-09-08, so
   this is close to binding. The backtest removed this refusal for the ladder path
   (`backtest-v6-5m.js:572-575`); live still has it.
