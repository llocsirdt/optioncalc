## Intent

Measure the **covering-POLICY axis** with geometry held at `tent`, so v0-vs-v3 is a clean A/B: should a
cover be rested the instant a position opens (v0), or only once the book or the position has *earned*
one? The stated case for arming (`index.js:166-176`; `placeContinuousCovers` comment, `trader.js:1921-1928`) is
the failure mode instant covering cannot see — a couple of deep winners sitting through a reversal while total
book risk stays low. v3 is the **only family where continuous covers do not go on at birth**.

## Open signal

Identical to v0-v2: `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`) — green + new
high → **bull**; red + new low → **bear**. Signal on /NQ, pricing on NDX, strikes by placement G.

## Cover signal

Same triggers as v0, but **continuous** is gated. Each pass, per filled uncovered position with no cover
working, a continuous cover is placed only if **either** arm holds (`placeContinuousCovers`,
`trader.js:1929-1958`):

| Arm | Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) book risk** | `−bookFloorNow ≥ 0.20 × lossTarget` | the governor's own measure of how close the day is to its working target |
| **(b) opportunity** | `W − openCost − coverMark ≥ 1.0 × coverMark` | this cover locks at least what it costs, regardless of book risk |

If neither holds, no order is placed that pass. A placement via arm (b) logs `cover-arm-opportunity`. Once
placed, the order life is the shared one: lock target `W − openCost − minLock`, cover ladder toward
break-even, stall cover after 15 min, give-up at 20 points through the short strike (README). At 20/40W the
cover's strike comes from the cover range (as on v0), and arm (b) is tested on that range pick's mark.

**`lossTarget` per width** is 1,400 / 2,100 / 5,250 (cATM 2,450 / 5,250), so arm (a) trips far earlier on 10W/20W
than under the $5,000 target the arms were tuned on (2026-09-06). On `-unc` twins there is no `lossTarget`, so arm
(a) can never trip and covering is opportunity-only (`index.js:1289-1293`, the `buildVariants` comment; same note
in `buildUncapped`).

Because arming leaves positions without a cover working, the **reversal** trigger can actually place
orders on v3, unlike v0-v2.

## Trend / reversal recognition

Identical to v0 — single 15m candle direction plus the prior-extreme break.

v3-10 is also named in `CAPPRES_LIVE` (`index.js:497`), but its 1×W preset is always overwritten by
`TUNED_CAPS` (`['v3-10', 2000]`, `index.js:752`).

**Why 0.20 / 1.0:** the 70-combination arming sweep (2026-09-06, `index.js:170-175`) found the opportunity arm
does nearly all the work and that a lower `oppRatio` is uniformly better ("the cheap covers are the ones that
pay"). That sweep ran under the old $5,000 `lossTarget`, the old minLock, and instant open fills.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **The arming sweep is stale.** It was measured under a $5,000 `lossTarget`; today's v3 targets are
   1,400-5,250, so arm (a) trips several times earlier on 10W/20W. Whether 0.20 is still the right fraction —
   or the risk arm should key off `lossMax` — has not been re-swept.
2. **Opportunity-only arming loses on the uncapped twins** (baselines above). Whether the opportunity arm alone
   is a viable policy, or only works because the risk arm backs it, is not recorded.
3. **Cover-to-stack parity bites here.** In the backtest a governor-blocked open locks deep winners (≥ 0.65×W)
   that have no cover working (`backtest-v6-5m.js` ~2064-2085, `coverToStackVsRisk` set by `opts-for.js:78`);
   live the governor branch just logs `open-skip-governor` (`trader.js:2459`) and cover-to-stack runs only when
   `softCap`/`hardCap`/`riskCap` block (`trader.js:2478-2510`) — all null on every variant, so it never fires.
   Only v3 routinely has uncovered positions for this to matter. Code-read, not measured.
4. **Stale comments:** `index.js:184-188` says "v1-v3 arm only on risk … 60% of lossTarget or … 2x its own cost" —
   only v3 arms, at 0.20 / 1.0. `index.js:193-194` says "v0-vs-v1 is a clean A/B of the POLICY" — it is v0-vs-v3
   (v0-vs-v1 is geometry).
5. **Intent not documented:** why the two arms are OR'd rather than staged (e.g. opportunity-only early,
   risk-armed late).
