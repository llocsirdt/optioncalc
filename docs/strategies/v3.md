## Intent

Measure the **covering-POLICY axis** with geometry held at `tent`, so v0-vs-v3 is a clean A/B: should a
cover be rested the instant a position opens (v0), or only once the book or the position has *earned*
one? The stated case for arming (`index.js:182-186`, `trader.js:1537-1544`) is the failure mode instant
covering cannot see — a couple of deep winners sitting through a reversal while total book risk stays
low. v3 is the **only family where continuous covers do not go on at birth**.

## Open signal

Identical to v0-v2: `classicSignal` at 15m closes only (`signals/classic-signal.js:7-24`) — green + new
high → **bull**; red + new low → **bear**. Signal on /NQ, pricing on NDX, strikes by placement G.

## Cover signal

Same triggers as v0, but **continuous** is gated. Each bar, per filled uncovered position with no cover
working, a continuous cover is placed only if **either** arm holds (`trader.js:1545-1563`; backtest
`backtest-v6-5m.js` a0b):

| Arm | Condition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) book risk** | `−bookFloorNow ≥ 0.20 × lossTarget` | the governor's own measure of how close the day is to its working target |
| **(b) opportunity** | `W − openCost − coverMark ≥ 1.0 × coverMark` | this cover locks at least what it costs, regardless of book risk |

If neither holds, no order is placed that bar. A placement via arm (b) logs `cover-arm-opportunity`.
Once placed, the order life is the shared one: lock target `W − openCost − minLock`, cover ladder to
break-even, give-up at 10 points through the short strike (README).

**Where arm (a) trips now.** `lossTarget` is no longer a fleet-wide $5,000; it is `0.7 × lossMax`, so with
v3's caps (2,000 / 3,000 / 7,500; cATM 3,500 / 7,500) the risk arm trips at a book floor of about
−$280 / −$420 / −$1,050 (cATM −$490 / −$1,050) — arithmetic from the config, not a measurement. That is
far earlier than the "$1,000 of $5,000" the arm was tuned under on 2026-09-06. On `-unc` twins there is
no `lossTarget`, so arm (a) can never trip and covering is opportunity-only (`index.js:1080-1082`).

Because arming leaves positions without a cover working, the **reversal** trigger can actually place
orders on v3, unlike v0-v2.

**Why 0.20 / 1.0:** the 70-combination arming sweep (2026-09-06, `index.js:164-174`) found the
opportunity arm does nearly all the work and that a lower `oppRatio` is uniformly better ("the cheap covers
are the ones that pay"). That sweep ran under the old $5,000 `lossTarget`, the old minLock, and instant
open fills.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **The arming sweep is stale.** It was measured under a $5,000 `lossTarget`; today's v3 targets are
   $1,400-$5,250, so arm (a) trips several times earlier on 10W/20W. Whether 0.20 is still the right
   fraction — or the risk arm should key off `lossMax` — has not been re-swept.
2. **Cover-to-stack parity (README #4) bites here.** In the backtest a governor-blocked open locks deep
   winners that have no cover working (`backtest-v6-5m.js:1289-1293`, resting lock covers); live the
   governor branch skips the open without trying (`trader.js:1712-1717`) and cover-to-stack only runs for a
   legacy cap v3 does not have. Only v3 routinely has uncovered positions for this to matter. Code-read,
   not measured.
3. **v3 is not the cover-prior-on-open ladder.** Arming *delays* the cover; the 2026-09-09 intent (a new
   open triggers covering a prior position, `coverPriorOnOpen`) is a different rule and remains
   backtest-only and unset (README #1).
4. **Intent not documented:** why the two arms are OR'd rather than staged (e.g. opportunity-only early,
   risk-armed late).
5. *Resolved since 2026-09-09:* the "silent no-cover trap" (`W − openCost − minLock ≤ 0` places no
   order, `trader.js:1550`) cannot bind on adaptive variants under placement G: the walk cap is at most
   0.60×W and minLock at most 0.20×W.
