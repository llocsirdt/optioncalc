## Intent

**v9 = v7 signal + proactive deep-ITM covering (0.80×W) + reopen after a quick cover**, and deliberately
**without cover-to-open-wait** — it is the control for that rule (`index.js:1074-1076`: "v6, v7 and v8 at every
width; v9 stays on today's rules as the control"). Since 2026-10-14 v8 is the same construction on the v6 signal
(`index.js:1071-1072`).

Since 2026-09-07 v9 is an **experiment slot**. User's ruling: *"we want to use those slots to see if we
can tweak v6 or v7 rules to beat them, which is likely why they're so closely correlated."* High
correlation with the parent is the point of a mutation slot — **do not propose retiring v9 for it**.

## Open signal

**Identical to [v7](v7.md#open-signal)** — `v7Signal` with `{ fiveMin: true, beWrong: true }` and
`bidirectional: true`, every 5m bar: per-side intra-5m cover/flip; at 15m closes per-side trend-flip →
**be-wrong** (open the opposite side without covering the underwater one) → overextension → grind →
active-cover → gated trend continuation.

Two engine-side differences from v7:
- **Reopen after a quick cover** (`reopenOnCover`) on **every** v9 variant; on v7 only v7-10 has it. Rule in
  [v7.md](v7.md#open-signal). Live only.
- **No cover-to-open-wait**: an open on an underwater side is placed as usual (subject to the governor), where
  v7 would escalate that side's covers to give-up and skip the open.

## Cover signal

v7's triggers (per side), plus:

| Trigger | Condition | Placed |
|---|---|---|
| **proactive** | uncovered position, no cover working, spread marking ≥ **0.80 × W** | break-even resting tent (`selectCoverFixedMark`, `trader.js:2310-2322`) |
| be-wrong | places **no cover** (by design) | — |
| shared layer | continuous cover (10W plateau `offset1` / minLock 0.05×W; 20/40W cover range / minLock 0.20×W), ladder, stall 15 min, give-up 20 pts, governor deferral, floor raise, wings, floor offsets | README |
| cover-to-stack | never fires live (no `softCap`/`hardCap`/`riskCap`); backtest-only on governor blocks — see [v8.md](v8.md#cover-signal) | — |

As with v8, continuous covering normally claims a position long before it is 0.80×W deep.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **v9 vs v7 is no longer a one-flag comparison.** It differs by proactive cover, reopen (at 20/40W and on every
   `-unc`/`-cATM`), and the missing cover-to-open-wait. v8 vs v6 differs by proactive + reopen only. Reading the
   proactive cover's effect alone needs the v8/v6 pair, or the backtest (where reopen is absent).
2. **Proactive at 0.80 "barely fires"** per the 2026-09 sweep on v8 (0.70 was that sweep's peak); v8 now sits at
   0.80 too, so neither family carries the measured peak.
3. **Stale comments / label:** `index.js:220-221` says v9 is "proactiveCover 0.80 on top of the $20k backstop"
   (no such backstop exists) and "still paper pending go/no-go" (no decision recorded); the label "be-wrong +
   caps" implies caps, and no v9 variant carries `softCap`/`hardCap`/`riskCap`. `family-about.js` calls it
   "be-wrong + proactive".
