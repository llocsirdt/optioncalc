# v5 — "trend-flip"

`{ key: 'v5', label: 'trend-flip', signalFn: at15(v5Signal), signalCfg: {}, ...PORTED_COVER }`

Signal: `server/src/candle-spread/signals/v5-signals.js`.

## Intent

Give v4 the **exit it does not have**. v4 covers only on mean-reversion wick-stabs, so it (a)
whipsaws off a correct trend on tiny counter-bounces and (b) gets stuck holding against a clean trend
with no way out — 3/26 held bull all day into a −300 pt downtrend, 8/14 held bear into a rising
close. v5 adds a *structural* read (15m midline + 9EMA) and covers the whole book the moment the
structural trend flips against it. `v5-signals.js:3-8`.

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`).

Identical cascade to v4, with one trigger inserted **at the top**:

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Trend-flip** (`v5-signals.js:63-67`) | holding a side while the opposite **structural trend** is confirmed: `close` **and** 9EMA both across the 15m midline against you | `openSide: <new side>, cover: true` — cover the whole book **and** take the new side |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`:79-90`) | as v4: wick reaches the 5m band in band-widths (`reversalFrac −0.15`) and closes back inside | open the reversal side, cover the opposite |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`:96-109`) | as v4: 1m band ≥ `widenFrac 0.10` beyond the 5m/15m channel + reversal candle | open the opposite side, cover the held |
| 3 | Candle at confluence (`:112-122`) | **OFF by default** | — |
| 4 | Active covering (`:128-138`) | as v4: held side into a ≥3-TF opposing cluster within 8 pts | cover all, go flat |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`:141-150`) | as v4: price + 9EMA on the same side of the 15m midline, matching candle colour, not paused into a cluster | open with the trend |

Two optional gates exist and both default OFF:
- `flipMomentum` (`:63`) — require a single-bar higher-high / lower-low to confirm the flip
- `revTrendGate` (`:78`) — suppress the counter-trend dip-buy / rip-sell inside a confirmed trend

## Cover signal

Same two layers as v4, plus the flip:

| Trigger | Condition | Placed | Fills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **trend-flip** (new) | structural trend crosses against the held book at a 15m close | resting order at `W − openCost` on every uncovered position, on that bar | when the real mark reaches it |
| overextension / grind reversal | as v4 | same | same |
| active-cover | held side into an opposing cluster | same | same |
| `continuous` (shared) | every 5m bar, unarmed → at birth | `W − openCost − 0.30×W` | same |
| `lock` (shared) | governor needs room | `W − openCost` | same |

The flip covers **the whole book** (`cover: true` → `coverSet = ['bull','bear']`,
`backtest-v6-5m.js:724`), unlike v7's per-side version.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend** (the new part): 15m `close` **and** 15m 9EMA on the same side of the 15m BB
  midline. Slow — a 20-bar (~5 h) average.
- **Trend has changed** = that pair crosses the midline against the held book. No overextension
  required, no momentum confirm.
- **Reversal** (unchanged from v4) = wick rejection at the 5m band, or 1m-band expansion + candle
  formation.
- **Momentum** (`higherHigh` / `lowerLow`, `:52-53`) is computed but only consumed by the
  default-off `flipMomentum` gate.

**v5 beats v4 at every width — +$639k at $20, +$921k at $40.** The largest single-step gain in the
lineage before v6.

From the original 87-day fork measurement (`project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`):
- terminal **+$155.7k vs v4's +$113.2k**, floor +$244.3k vs +$202.9k, **max drawdown $9.7k vs $23.7k
  (more than halved)**, worst day −$7.7k vs −$9.1k. Robust in *and* out of sample.
- 3/26 went −$8,670 → **+$2,110**. 8/14 went −$9,121 → −$7,725 (still the hardest chop day).
- **Calibration surprise:** the user's own v1-v3 "trend persists while it keeps making new
  highs/lows" rule, encoded as `flipMomentum` and `revTrendGate`, **both hurt** — they only delay
  good exits and drop reversal covers whose tents still lock floor. `revTrendGate`: terminal $111k vs
  $156k, drawdown doubled. The slow midline+9EMA cross already encodes trend persistence; the
  single-bar break is too noisy a proxy.
- The pure structural flip wins. Both gates default off but are kept as flags for future
  re-encoding.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **CONFIRMED (all families):** continuous covering pre-empts the trend-flip cover. v5's defining
   mechanism places a `reversal`-tagged order only on positions **without** a pending cover — and
   under unarmed continuous covering there are none. Live 2026-09-08: **0 of 101 covers came from
   the reversal path.** *The mechanism that defines v5 does not fire live.*
   In backtest it still shapes P&L, because the flip also sets `st.dir = 'none'` and drives the
   opposite open (`backtest-v6-5m.js:750`) — so v5-vs-v4 remains a valid backtest comparison, but the
   *cover* half of the flip is inert whenever a continuous cover already rests.
2. **`minLock 0.30` for v5 is inferred, not measured** — `index.js:279` says explicitly that v5's
   value comes from being "v6's lineage predecessor". Treat as unvalidated.
3. **Intent not documented:** whether the flip should cover the whole book or only the wrong-way
   side. v7 later chose per-side, but no note records that as a correction to v5 rather than a
   consequence of bidirectionality.
4. **Not built (from the v5 to-do list in the narrative):** reading the same extreme/reversal
   patterns on the 5m and 15m candles against *their own* bands+9EMA rather than leaning on 1m; and
   9EMA-as-directional-resistance as a trend-continuation confirm. Both were listed as the next v5
   ideas; neither exists in `v5-signals.js`.
