## Intent

Give v4 the **exit it does not have**. v4 covers only on mean-reversion wick-stabs, so it whipsaws off a
correct trend on small counter-bounces and gets stuck holding against a clean trend (3/26 held bull all
day into a −300 pt downtrend; 8/14 held bear into a rising close). v5 adds a *structural* read (15m midline
+ 9EMA) and covers the whole book the moment the structural trend flips against it (`v5-signals.js:3-8`).

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`). Identical cascade to v4, with one trigger inserted **at the top**:

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | **Trend-flip** (`v5-signals.js:63-67`) | holding a side while the opposite **structural trend** is confirmed: `close` **and** 9EMA both across the 15m midline against you | `openSide: <new side>, cover: true` — cover the whole book **and** take the new side |
| 1 | Overextension reversal (`:79-90`) | as v4 (`reversalFrac −0.15`, close back inside) | open the reversal side, cover the opposite |
| 2 | Grind top/bottom (`:96-109`) | as v4 (`widenFrac 0.10` + reversal candle) | open the opposite side, cover the held |
| 3 | Candle at confluence (`:112-122`) | **OFF by default** | — |
| 4 | Active covering (`:128-138`) | as v4 (≥3-TF opposing cluster within 8 pts) | cover all, go flat |
| 5 | Gated trend continuation (`:141-150`) | as v4 | open with the trend |

Two optional gates, both default OFF: `flipMomentum` (`:63`, single-bar HH/LL confirm) and `revTrendGate`
(`:78`, suppress the counter-trend dip-buy/rip-sell inside a confirmed trend).

## Cover signal

The flip covers **the whole book** (`cover: true` → both sides, `trader.js:1412-1414`), unlike v7's
per-side version, and resets the stance so the new side can open. Signal covers (flip, overext, grind,
active-cover) place break-even resting orders on positions **without** a cover working; the shared
continuous cover (minLock by width, laddered to break-even), give-up and governor deferral apply on top
(README). In practice continuous covering claims most positions first (README #1), so the flip's
*cover* half rarely places an order while its *flip* half — stance reset, opposite open, cancelling a
working open on the wrong side — always acts.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Structural trend** (the new part): 15m `close` **and** 15m 9EMA on the same side of the 15m BB
  midline. Slow — a 20-bar (~5 h) average.
- **Trend has changed** = that pair crosses the midline against the held book. No momentum confirm.
- **Reversal** (unchanged from v4) = wick rejection at the 5m band, or 1m-band expansion + candle.
- `higherHigh` / `lowerLow` (`:52-53`) are computed but only feed the default-off `flipMomentum` gate.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **The trend-flip's cover half is pre-empted by continuous covering** (README #1). The flip still drives
   the stance and the opposite open, so v5-vs-v4 remains a meaningful comparison, but the cover it was
   built to place is mostly not the one that closes the position.
2. **Intent not documented:** whether the flip should cover the whole book or only the wrong-way side.
   v7 later chose per-side; no note records that as a correction to v5.
3. **Not built from the v5 to-do list:** reading the extreme/reversal patterns on the 5m and 15m candles
   against their own bands rather than leaning on 1m; 9EMA-as-resistance as a continuation confirm.
