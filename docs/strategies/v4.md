## Intent

Encode **the user's actual discretionary edge**. From `project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`: 16 lines on
one price axis (1/5/15/60m × BB-upper, BB-mid/20-SMA, BB-lower, 9EMA); clustered lines from multiple
timeframes = stronger support/resistance; a break-and-close-through runs to the next line; the *fast band
extending beyond the slow channel* marks a local top or bottom. v0-v3 "blindly open every trend candle +
cover on any reversal"; v4 uses the line map to decide when to pause opens, when to fade an extreme early,
and where covers are likely (`v4-signals.js:2-22`).

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`, `index.js:95`). The signal reads a multi-TF snapshot `A`
(1m/5m/15m/60m bands + 9EMA) built from **/NQ 24h**; strikes and pricing come from cash NDX (placement G).

Strict priority order; first match returns (`v4Signal`, `v4-signals.js:31-128`):

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overextension reversal** (`:46-57`) | 15m wick reaches/passes the **5m** band, measured in 5m band-widths (`reversalFrac` default **−0.15**), **and the candle closes back inside** (rejection) | open **bull** at a bottom / **bear** at a top; cover the opposite side if held |
| 2 | **Grind top/bottom** (`:59-76`) | 1m band pushes ≥ `widenFrac` (**0.10**) of its own width beyond the 5m/15m channel, price on the extended side of the 15m midline, **and** a reversal candle prints | open the opposite side, covering the held side |
| 3 | **Candle at confluence** (`:78-89`) | reversal candle at a ≥3-timeframe cluster | **OFF by default** (`candleConfluenceRadius = null`) |
| 4 | **Active covering** (`:91-105`) | held side, price on its side of the 15m midline, strong (≥3-TF) opposing cluster within `activeCoverRadius` (**8 pts**) | `openSide: null, cover: true` — cover all, go flat |
| 5 | **Gated trend continuation** (`:107-121`) | 15m `close` **and** 9EMA on the same side of the 15m midline, candle colour matches, not holding the opposite side — **unless** a strong cluster sits within `pausePts` (**8**) ahead, or (off by default) `htfGate` | open **with** the trend, or pause |
| 6 | Green-breaks-high (`:123-125`) | `cfg.breakOpen` | **OFF by default** |

Fallthrough: `neutral`. Trigger 1 is a *rejection* rule: a close beyond the band is continuation. A signal for
the other side, or a cover of a side, also cancels a still-working open.

## Cover signal

**(a) From the signal.** Triggers 1/2/3 set `cover: held === <opposite>`; trigger 4 covers without opening.
`cover: true` means **both sides**. A signal cover places a break-even resting **tent** (`selectCoverFixedMark`;
`fixed-mark` ignores `coverGeometry`) on positions **without** a cover already working.

**(b) From the shared machinery** (README): continuous cover on every position from the first pass after it
fills, at `W − openCost − minLock`, laddered toward break-even; stall cover at 15 min; give-up at 20 points through
the short strike; governor deferral. Because (b) runs first, (a) mostly shapes the stance and cancels working
opens rather than placing covers.

**10W plateau cover** (`applyPlateauCover`, `index.js:909-914`; default for every 10W variant of v4 and up since
2026-10-14, `-unc` included): the continuous cover rests at `offset1` — its short leg one strike beyond the
position's short on the profit side (`spread-logic.coverShortFor`, `:191-199`) — so open + cover form a 10-point
condor plateau instead of a butterfly peak; minLock drops to **0.05×W** so it fills sooner; a give-up moves the
cover back to the tent (`giveUpGeometry: 'tent'`). At 20/40W the cover range applies instead (9 inside / 2
beyond / 0.02×W, give-up re-strikes `inside`).

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Trend** = *structural*: 15m `close` **and** 15m 9EMA on the same side of the 15m BB midline
  (`v4-signals.js:107-109`).
- **Reversal** = wick rejection at the 5m band (volatility-relative), 1m-band expansion beyond the slower
  channel confirmed by a candle formation, or (off) a reversal candle at a cluster.
- **v4 has no exit for a trend that simply turns against it** — the gap v5 fixes.
- **Confluence** = ≥3 lines from ≥3 distinct timeframes (`signals/confluence.js`), used to pause opens and
  to cover into.

## Open questions / discrepancies

1. **Continuous covering pre-empts `active-cover`**, the trigger built to reproduce the user's
   discretionary covering. The mechanism is in the signal but rarely places an order.
2. **Comment vs code:** the `v4-signals.js` header (`:24-25`) says signals (2)-(4) "are OFF unless their cfg gate
   is set"; the code defaults `widenFrac 0.10` and `activeCoverRadius 8`, so grind and active cover are **on**
   and only (3) is off. Same header in `v5`/`v6`/`v7-signals.js`.
3. **`activeCoverRadius 8` was shipped pending user confirmation**; none is recorded.
4. **Plateau cover was adopted against the backtest.** The `applyPlateauCover` comment (`index.js:895-900`) records
   the 821-day read as somewhat worse than the tent; the user chose it because live ran far below the backtest
   either way. Settled — not to be reverted.
5. **Intent gap: 15m gating.** The narrative says the user acts *mid-candle* off the 5m; v4 deliberately
   does not (v6 does).
6. **Never built from the narrative:** sizing by how many lines align (every open is `quantity: 1`), and a
   distinct role for the 60m timeframe (beyond the default-off `htfGate`).
