## Intent

Encode **the user's actual discretionary edge** as a strategy. From
`project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`: 16 lines on one price axis (1/5/15/60m × BB-upper, BB-mid/20-SMA,
BB-lower, 9EMA), where clustered lines from multiple timeframes = stronger support/resistance, a
break-and-close-through runs to the next line, and the *fast band extending beyond the slow channel*
marks a local top or bottom. v0-v3 "blindly open every trend candle + cover on any reversal"; v4 uses
the line map to decide when to pause opens, when to fade an extreme early, and where covers are
likely.

The file header (`v4-signals.js:2-26`) states it as "the multi-timeframe precision layer …
replacing v0-v3's single-timeframe green-breaks-high / any-reversal rules."

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`, `index.js:63`). The signal reads a multi-TF snapshot `A`
(1m/5m/15m/60m bands + 9EMA) built from **/NQ 24h** data; strikes and pricing come from cash NDX.

Evaluated in strict priority order; the first match returns (`v4Signal`, `v4-signals.js:31-122`):

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overextension reversal** (`v4-signals.js:48-57`) | 15m wick reaches/passes the **5m** band, measured in 5m-band-widths (`(bblower−low)/bw5 ≥ reversalFrac`, default **−0.15**), **and the candle closes back inside** the band (rejection) | open **bull** at a bottom / **bear** at a top, covering the opposite side if held |
| 2 | **Grind top/bottom** (`:63-76`) | 1m band pushes ≥ `widenFrac` (default **0.10**) of its own width beyond the 5m/15m channel, price on the extended side of the 15m midline, **and** a reversal candle prints (`candle-patterns.bearish/bullishReversalCandle`) | open the opposite side, covering the held side |
| 3 | **Candle at confluence** (`:79-89`) | reversal candle right at a ≥3-timeframe cluster within `candleConfluenceRadius` | open the opposite side. **OFF by default** (`ccRadius = null`) |
| 4 | **Active covering** (`:95-105`) | a held side, price on its side of the 15m midline, and a strong (≥3-TF) opposing cluster within `activeCoverRadius` (default **8 pts**) | `openSide: null, cover: true` — cover all, go flat |
| 5 | **Gated trend continuation** (`:107-119`) | 15m `close` **and** 9EMA on the same side of the 15m midline, candle colour matches, and not already holding the opposite side — **unless** a strong ≥3-TF cluster sits within `pausePts` (default **8**) ahead | open **with** the trend; or pause |

Fallthrough: `neutral`, no action.

Note trigger 1 is a *rejection* rule, not a stab rule: a close beyond the band is continuation, not a
reversal (`v4-signals.js:46-47`).

## Cover signal

Two independent layers.

**(a) From the signal.** `v4Signal` returns `cover: true` from triggers 1-4:
- triggers 1/2/3 set `cover: held === <opposite>` — a reversal both flips and covers
- trigger 4 (`active-cover`) covers **without** opening: "lock the tent when a HELD trend runs into a
  strong opposing confluence, without a full reversal (cover all held, go flat, re-engage next
  signal). This is what the user does discretionarily" (`v4-signals.js:17-20`).

A signal cover places resting orders at `W − openCost` on every uncovered position **that does not
already have a cover working** (`backtest-v6-5m.js:722-751`, `trader.js:708-742`), tagged `reversal`.

**(b) From the shared machinery.** `continuous` (every 5m bar, unarmed → at birth, target
`W − openCost − 0.30×W`) and `lock` (cover-to-continue at the governor's ceiling). See
[README.md](README.md#the-five-cover-triggers-shared-by-all-families).

**When placed vs when filled:** all v4 covers are *resting* orders. They are placed on the bar the
trigger fires and fill only on a later bar when the real mark reaches the limit. Nothing is booked at
placement.

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Trend** = *structural*: 15m `close` **and** the 15m 9EMA both on the same side of the 15m BB
  midline (20-SMA) — `v4-signals.js:108-109`.
- **Reversal** = one of three independent reads, in priority order: a wick rejection at the 5m band
  (volatility-relative), a 1m-band expansion beyond the slower channel confirmed by a candle
  formation, or (off by default) a reversal candle at a line cluster.
- **Direction has changed** only via those reversal triggers. **v4 has no exit for a trend that
  simply turns against it** — that gap is exactly what v5 was built to fix.
- **Confluence** = clusters of ≥3 lines from ≥3 distinct timeframes (`signals/confluence.js`), used
  as S/R context: pause opens into it, cover into it.

## Lineage

- **Inherits from:** nothing in the v0-v3 group — v4 is a fresh signal built from the user's
  annotated charts (`project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`, 2026-08-28/29).
- **Frozen** as the baseline v5 forks from; the file is not to be changed
  (`v5-signals.js:3` — "v4 is frozen as the baseline").
- **Three calibrated fixes** during its build, all recorded: (1) the fixed "15 pts beyond the 5m
  band" threshold did not transfer across volatility regimes → replaced by the band-width-relative
  `reversalFrac`; (2) grind tops were missed because the 5m band rises with price → the `widenFrac`
  trigger; (3) covers were too sparse (v4 only covered at the rare opposite extreme) → active
  covering.

## Findings

Committed baseline (765 days):

| variant | total $ | worst day $ | win % | opens/day | maxDD30 $ | ret/maxDD30 | peak capital $ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v4-10 | 1,128,111 | −5,995 | 70% | 15.8 | −16,376 | 68.9 | 1,585 |
| v4-20 | 1,641,262 | −7,000 | 61% | 17.7 | −34,123 | 48.1 | 3,342 |
| v4-40 | 1,638,106 | −9,000 | 49% | 17.6 | −75,995 | 21.6 | 7,146 |

From the original 87-day validation (`project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`, in- and out-of-sample):
- **Every v4 config decisively beat classic on BOTH floor and terminal** — classic's terminal was
  *negative*. Core thesis validated.
- `reversalFrac` sweet spot is broad (−0.20 … −0.15 all beat classic) → not a knife-edge overfit.
- **Grind (`widenFrac`) is a robust floor-booster** (+$12k floor over 87 days, both samples) at a
  small noisy-terminal cost.
- **Active covering is ~neutral in aggregate** — helped in-sample, washed out-of-sample.
  `activeCoverRadius 8` is called "the least-validated / most overfit-prone knob"; kept on because it
  matches the user's real discretionary behaviour. **This was flagged as an open judgment call to
  confirm with the user and no confirmation is recorded.**
- **⭐ The biggest insight from the v4 work:** average *floor* ($2,444/day) exceeded average
  *terminal* ($1,364/day). The naked book was a net loser and the sole source of drawdown. The edge
  lives in the covering.
- Continuous covering turned v4-20 from **−$12,479 to +$162,870** at `minLock 0.25W` (120 days) —
  the largest relative beneficiary of that change.
- v4's two disaster days (3/26 −$8.7k, 8/14 −$9.1k) share one root cause: it only covers/flips on
  wick-stab reversals, so it whipsaws on tiny counter-bounces and gets **stuck holding against a
  clean trend with no exit**. → v5.

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **CONFIRMED (all families):** continuous covering rests a cover at birth and starves the
   `reversal` trigger — including v4's `active-cover`, the trigger explicitly built to reproduce the
   user's discretionary covering. Live 2026-09-08: 0 of 101 covers came from any non-continuous
   trigger. The mechanism v4 exists for is measured but not exercised.
2. **Unresolved by design:** `activeCoverRadius 8` was shipped on with an explicit note that the user
   should confirm it. No confirmation is recorded anywhere.
3. **Intent gap: `v4Signal` is wrapped in `at15`, but the engine steps every 5m.** The v4 narrative
   states the user acts *mid-candle* off the 5m ("overextension … even MID-CANDLE — the reason 5m is
   watched, not just 15m closes"). v4 deliberately does not, and v6 is the family that fixed it — but
   nothing in v4's own definition records that the 15m gating is a known limitation rather than the
   design.
4. **Never built from the narrative:** the "size by how many lines align / how far the fast band is
   below them" rule. Every v4 open is `quantity: 1` (`index.js:80`). *Intent documented, not
   implemented.*
5. **Never built:** the 60m timeframe's role ("big-picture confluence map vs entry gate" — listed as
   an open threshold in the narrative). `confluence.js` includes 60m lines in clusters, but no rule
   treats 60m distinctly.
