## Intent

Encode **the user's actual discretionary edge**. From `project_v4_multitimeframe_strategy`: 16 lines on
one price axis (1/5/15/60m × BB-upper, BB-mid/20-SMA, BB-lower, 9EMA); clustered lines from multiple
timeframes = stronger support/resistance; a break-and-close-through runs to the next line; the *fast band
extending beyond the slow channel* marks a local top or bottom. v0-v3 "blindly open every trend candle +
cover on any reversal"; v4 uses the line map to decide when to pause opens, when to fade an extreme early,
and where covers are likely (`v4-signals.js:2-26`).

## Open signal

Timeframe: **15m closes only** (`at15`, `index.js:93`). The signal reads a multi-TF snapshot `A`
(1m/5m/15m/60m bands + 9EMA) built from **/NQ 24h**; strikes and pricing come from cash NDX (placement G).

Strict priority order; first match returns (`v4Signal`, `v4-signals.js:31-122`):

| # | Trigger | Condition | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Overextension reversal** (`:48-57`) | 15m wick reaches/passes the **5m** band, measured in 5m band-widths (`reversalFrac` default **−0.15**), **and the candle closes back inside** (rejection) | open **bull** at a bottom / **bear** at a top; cover the opposite side if held |
| 2 | **Grind top/bottom** (`:63-76`) | 1m band pushes ≥ `widenFrac` (**0.10**) of its own width beyond the 5m/15m channel, price on the extended side of the 15m midline, **and** a reversal candle prints | open the opposite side, covering the held side |
| 3 | **Candle at confluence** (`:79-89`) | reversal candle at a ≥3-timeframe cluster | **OFF by default** (`candleConfluenceRadius = null`) |
| 4 | **Active covering** (`:95-105`) | held side, price on its side of the 15m midline, strong (≥3-TF) opposing cluster within `activeCoverRadius` (**8 pts**) | `openSide: null, cover: true` — cover all, go flat |
| 5 | **Gated trend continuation** (`:107-119`) | 15m `close` **and** 9EMA on the same side of the 15m midline, candle colour matches, not holding the opposite side — **unless** a strong cluster sits within `pausePts` (**8**) ahead | open **with** the trend, or pause |

Fallthrough: `neutral`. Trigger 1 is a *rejection* rule: a close beyond the band is continuation
(`:46-47`). A signal for the other side, or a cover of a side, also cancels a still-working open.

## Cover signal

**(a) From the signal.** Triggers 1/2/3 set `cover: held === <opposite>`; trigger 4 covers without
opening — "what the user does discretionarily" (`v4-signals.js:17-20`). `cover: true` means **both sides**
(`trader.js:1412-1414`). A signal cover places a break-even resting order (`W − openCost`) on positions
**without** a cover already working.

**(b) From the shared machinery** (README): `continuous` (unarmed → on every position from the first
candle after it fills, at `W − openCost − minLock`, laddered to break-even), give-up at 10 points through
the short strike, governor deferral. Because (b) runs first, (a) mostly shapes the stance and cancels
working opens rather than placing covers (README #1).

## Trend / reversal recognition

- **Trend** = *structural*: 15m `close` **and** 15m 9EMA on the same side of the 15m BB midline
  (`v4-signals.js:108-109`).
- **Reversal** = wick rejection at the 5m band (volatility-relative), 1m-band expansion beyond the slower
  channel confirmed by a candle formation, or (off) a reversal candle at a cluster.
- **v4 has no exit for a trend that simply turns against it** — the gap v5 fixes.
- **Confluence** = ≥3 lines from ≥3 distinct timeframes (`signals/confluence.js`), used to pause opens and
  to cover into.

Signal-design findings (87-day NDX validation, 2026-08-29; the lineage held on 762 NQ days):
- Every v4 config beat classic on BOTH floor and terminal. `reversalFrac` sweet spot broad (−0.20 … −0.15).
- **Grind (`widenFrac`) is a robust floor-booster.** **Active covering ~neutral in aggregate**;
  `activeCoverRadius 8` called "the least-validated / most overfit-prone knob", kept because it matches
  the user's discretionary behaviour.
- **The edge lives in the covering:** average floor exceeded average terminal; the naked book was the
  sole source of drawdown.
- v4's disaster days (3/26, 8/14) share one cause — no exit when stuck against a clean trend → v5.

Later:
- **minLock re-sweep (2026-10-04):** capped v4-10 ret/DD 239 at 0.10 vs 160 at 0.20 — part of the evidence
  for the width rule.
- **v4 > v7 at the same width is partly by design** — v4's capped shapes carry flies, v7's are the no-fly
  control (experiments log, 2026-09-30).

## Open questions / suspected drift

1. **Continuous covering pre-empts `active-cover`**, the trigger built to reproduce the user's
   discretionary covering (README #1). The mechanism is in the signal but rarely places an order.
2. **`activeCoverRadius 8` was shipped pending user confirmation**; none is recorded.
3. **Intent gap: 15m gating.** The narrative says the user acts *mid-candle* off the 5m; v4 deliberately
   does not (v6 does). Not recorded in v4's own definition as a known limitation.
4. **Never built from the narrative:** sizing by how many lines align (every open is `quantity: 1`), and a
   distinct role for the 60m timeframe.
