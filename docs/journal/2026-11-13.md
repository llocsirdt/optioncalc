## Trailing give-up (user idea)
The user's observation: give-ups cost us when the market recovers or chops. Clarified with the user:
- "Lowering" means the cover got cheaper after we raised to chase it.
- Live pins the give-up price, and the backtest used to let it fall.

The design agreed: after a give-up, trail the falling mark (mark + trail). A bounce fills it; it floors at the normal
cover price. Built as runDay5m opts.giveUpTrail, with a sweep of 0.025 / 0.05 / 0.10 x W running.
