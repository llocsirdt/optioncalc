## Market study E: run sizes and the "40-80 pt chop" reversal idea (study-fast-moves-reversals.js --only E)
User: small-TF chop runs are 40-80 pt; once a run hits that (esp. at a band / confluence) a reversal is likely; 80-120
on strong moves. Zigzag on 1m closes (theta 15/20/25), 731 NQ-proxy + 63 NDX days, drift/IV-matched placebo.
- Run sizes match the user's eye: theta 20 median 44 pt; 37% end at 40-80, 12% at 80-120, 7.5% above 120.
- But the reversal hazard does NOT rise at 40-80 — it FALLS as a run grows: -0.7pp at 40 to -5.1pp at 120 (z -4.7,
  negative ~every year). Band touch / confluence add nothing (40-60 + touch + confluence>=2: -0.8pp, z -0.7);
  tagging the 60m band is mildly NEGATIVE for a fade.
- Speed is the real variable: runs reaching 40 pt in ~4 min reach 80 51.5% / 120 23.1%; slow (12 min) 25.2% / 7.9%.
  Only weak fade cell: slow runs at X=40 (+1.4pp, z 2.1).
=> In aggregate the market does not reverse at bands after 40-80 pt runs; any v12 other-way open is risk placement,
best after a confirmed close-based 20-25 pt pullback; fast runs = hold with-trend risk.
