## Q: v9sg vs v9ob — where do they differ? (answered in chat)
v9ob (openTrendBlock 'A'): drops ANY open, first or add, against the trend, where trend = the 15m structural trend (close and
9EMA on the same side of the BB mid) AND the last completed hourly candle agreeing; neutral when they disagree.
v9sg (stackGate nqNotAgainst): judges only ADDS (a same-side uncovered position or working open already exists); blocks if
EITHER the NQ 15m or 60m 9EMA read (5m close vs that EMA, confirmed by the candle's own close) points against the side.
First opens are never gated. Broad scope + strict trigger (ob) vs narrow scope + easy trigger (sg).
