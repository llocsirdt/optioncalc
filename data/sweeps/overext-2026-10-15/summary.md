Generated 2026-10-16T01:17:46.102Z by scripts/candle-spread/study-overextension.js (analysis only, no engine change).

**Method.** Live roster config (buildRuns) -> optsFor -> runDay5m, openFillModel ladder, subBarFill 1m, recordReplay, over the 821-day combined dataset (tradeable days). Every directional open is joined to its SIGNAL bar's /NQ analysis snapshot. `pctB<tf>` = current price's %B on that TF oriented to the trade (1.00 = at the band ahead, 1.10 = 10% of band width beyond it). Outcome: good = covered at >= minLock; covlow = covered below minLock (not give-up); giveup = give-up cover; tail = uncovered at the close. "bad" = giveup + tail. pnl = the position's own settled P&L (real engine values). **Fit on 2023-2024, test on 2025-2026.** Objective = first-order P&L saved = -(sum pnl of removed opens), min removal 3% of the train population.

**Caveats.** First-order counterfactual only — removing an open frees governor headroom / changes cover-on-open and later opens; book-level effect needs a real engine gate + sweep. Backtest fills are mid-priced. The 2023-25 /NQ series is back-adjusted (fine for intraday bands). Population = opens WITH the 60m structural trend unless noted.

1. **Graded "beyond the band" overextension does NOT separate bad with-trend opens, on any TF, in any combination.**
   Bad% (give-up + tail) is ~45% (10W) / ~54% (20W) in every pctB bucket; opens far BEYOND the 60m band
   (pctB >= 1.25) are if anything slightly *better* (band-walk continuation), not worse. Every fitted single-TF,
   per-TF OR, and count>=k rule (1m/5m/15m/60m thresholds, % of band width or points) that looked good on 2023-24
   either costs money on 2025-26 or is indistinguishable from removing the same number of opens at random
   (p 0.07-0.6). The best train fits saved only ~$2-4k over two years in-sample — there was nothing to find.
2. **What weakly holds is the opposite zone: AT the 60m band, not through it** ("little room left" — matches the
   live review's 8-pt median room). A-priori rule `60m pctB in [0.9,1.0)` (with-60m-trend opens): removed opens
   earn ~$0-15/open vs ~$35-50 kept, bad% +4-6 pts, in 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 for v7-10; p=0.025 (v7-10) /
   0.002 (v6-10) vs random on the test years; but for v7-20 it is not significant (p=0.13) and 2024/2026 reverse.
   It is a *weak* edge: first-order P&L saved is ~zero (removed opens are break-even, not losers), so any value
   has to come from book effects (freed governor headroom, fewer tails near the cap) — that needs a real gate+sweep.
3. Not worth an engine gate as "overextension"; if anything is swept, sweep the zone gate below.
