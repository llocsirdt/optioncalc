## D. Findings and candidate v12 rules (written by hand after the run)

**Findings**
1. Jump size is driven by TIME OF DAY, not trend. Against-trend move p95 within 5 min (pts@29,556): 91 for 09:30-10:00, 73 for 10-11, 50 for 11-14, 48 for 14-15, 51 for 15-16. Midday is about half the open.
2. Trend state barely changes jump size. A with-trend jump (the one that hurts risk held against the trend) is no bigger than an against-trend jump: the 5-min p95 is 47 vs 50 at 11-14 and 88 vs 91 at the open. Against-trend and down tails are 5-15% fatter at p99. Mixed (15m/60m disagree) is about the same as trend.
3. Big moves happen every day. 98.9% of days have a >=50-pt move inside some 5-min window, 54.9% have a >=100-pt one and 18.5% have a >=150-pt one (pts@REF). Per decision minute: P(>=50 in 5m) = 14.4%, P(>=100 in 5m) = 2.0%, P(>=100 in 15m) = 9.9%.
4. Regime matters more than anything else. 2025 (tariff spring) 5-min p99 was 163 pts@REF, against 103/107/106 for 2023/2024/NDX 2026. A fixed budget is wrong by 1.5x in a high-vol year, so scale the budget with vol (the band-width IV, or realized vol).
5. Validation: the NQ proxy overstates NDX slightly. On the same 2026 days, the 5-min p95 is 69.0 for NQ vs 65.4 for NDX, and the 1-min rate of >=25-pt moves is 9.8% vs 6.8%, because NQ 1m bars are noisier while the index is smoothed. The two converge at 15-30 min (within 3%). So a budget sized on NQ is slightly conservative.
6. Jumps half-retrace often but fully retrace about half the time. Episodes of >=50 pts in 5 min (n=15,330, about 19/day): 45% fully retrace within 15 min, 57% within 30 min, and 76% half-retrace within 15 min. Jumps of >=100 pts retrace less (full30 52.7%). Do not assume a jump will come back.
7. No reversal detector predicts a turn beyond drift and context. Lift is measured against all bars matched on year x tf x 30-min bucket x 60m trend x direction. Of 25 detector x TF rows, the best is 15m bbCloseBackIn at +1.5pp on P(+50 before -25), z 1.3. Only 2/25 have z>2 in absolute value, and both are NEGATIVE. This matches the 09-09 "band-break + reversal: no edge" result.
8. Long runs persist, so fading them loses. A doji after a run of 3+ candles gives -2.9pp, z -3.5, negative in all 4 years (5m). A colour flip after 5+ same-colour 5m bars gives -2.7pp, z -2.4, negative in all 4 years. The old trend usually resumes.
9. Consistent but weak signals: bbCloseBackIn (5m and 15m, positive in all 4 years, +1.1/+1.5pp), trendFlip15 (positive in all 4 years, +1.2pp, z 1.1), and 15m bbCloseBackIn bull at a level against the 60m trend (+3.6pp, z 1.7, positive in all 4 years, n=440). v7-overext fires on 70% of 15m bars and shows -0.8pp. The "probe first" rate after a hammer/pin is 32% vs 48% on a random bar, so a hammer's extreme usually holds for one bar.
10. Multi-TF confirmation does not make adds safer on average: count 0/1/2/3 gives +0.5/+0.6/+1.0/+0.3pp lift on P(+50 before -25). The only pattern is three agreeing TFs in a MATURE trend (90m+) or above the 60m band (%B>1). Those give +2.7 to +2.9pp, positive in all 4 years, but z only 1.3-1.8. Three agreeing TFs EARLY (<90m) or inside the band gives -2.4 to -6pp. "Late in trend" therefore does NOT hurt; band-walk continuation is mildly real.
11. After one of the rarer reversal signals, jump risk is about 7-15% higher (P(>=50 in 5m) 15.2% vs 13.2%, 5m p95 77 vs 72).

**Candidate v12 rules (evidence strength)**
- R1 jump budget [STRONG, n=310k minutes]: size against-trend exposure so that a move of X loses no more than tolerance. X by time of day: 15-min p95 is 150/120/85/80/85 pts and 15-min p99 is 235/190/155/160/160 pts for 09:30-10/10-11/11-14/14-15/15-16. Use the same X for both sides (trend does not shrink it), and scale it by current IV / median IV.
- R2 reversal triggers [WEAK]: no trigger has edge as an OPEN signal. If v12 must act on one, use 15m bbCloseBackIn (best everywhere it appears), preferably at a level and against the 60m trend. Never trigger on doji-after-run or flip5 (they are anti-signals). Treat the "other-direction" open as risk placement (tents) rather than a prediction.
- R3 adds [WEAK]: do not require more TF confirmations; count is uninformative. If gating at all, avoid adding on a 3-TF burst in the first 90 min of a trend or inside the 60m band. Adds while beyond the 60m band in a mature trend are no worse than baseline and mildly better.
- R4 jump does not mean reversal [MODERATE]: about 45% of 50-pt jumps fully retrace within 15 min, which is no better than a coin flip. Don't cover INTO a jump expecting a snap-back, and don't treat a jump as a trend signal either.

## E. Swing structure and the hazard of a run ending (follow-up, 2026-10-17)

### E6 findings and revised trigger (written by hand; a rerun of `--only E` regenerates E1-E5 and drops this block)
- The swing structure matches the user's eye. On a 20-pt close zigzag the median run is 44 pts; 43.5% of runs are <40, 36.9% are 40-80, 12.2% are 80-120 and 7.5% are 120+. That is the same in every year, a little larger at 10-11 and in high IV.
- But the hazard of a run ending does NOT rise once it reaches 40-80. P(reverse 25 before extending 20) falls as the run grows: 43.2/42.4/41.4/40.3/38.5% at X=40/60/80/100/120, against a 43.9% placebo. The lifts are -0.7/-1.4/-2.3/-3.4/-5.1pp (z -1.7 to -4.7, negative in nearly every year), and the result holds at theta 15 and 25. Runs have momentum: the bigger they get, the more likely they continue.
- Band touch and confluence add nothing. "40-60 & touch & conf>=2" gives -0.8pp (z -0.7, n=2,155). Tagging the 60m band at 40-60 is negative (-1.8 to -2.2pp, z -2.4/-2.5, negative in all years at both X=40 and X=60).
- Speed is the real variable, and it predicts CONTINUATION. Fast runs at the 40-pt mark (top third, median 4 min to 40) reach 80 51.5% of the time and 120 23.1%; slow ones (median 12 min) reach 80 25.2% and 120 7.9%. At X=60/80 fast runs reverse first -4.0/-4.6pp (z -4.1/-3.6). Slow runs at 40 are the only mildly reversal-prone cell (+1.4pp, z 2.1, positive in 3 of 4 years).
- Revised v12 rule [STRONG as a NEGATIVE result]: do NOT open the other direction just because a run hit 40-80 + band + confluence. That is a coin flip at best and below the placebo at 80+. Use run speed as a hold/extend signal: when a run's pace to 40 is fast (≤ ~4-5 min), expect 80-120 and keep with-trend risk on. Place the other-direction tent only after an actual zigzag reversal is confirmed (a close-based retrace of ≥20-25 pts), as risk placement rather than a prediction. Any edge is at best WEAK: slow runs (12+ min to 40) at a 15m band tag.
