## 5. What to backtest (ranked)

Ranked by (mechanism makes sense) x (two-week evidence) x (not just fitting the 17 tail losers). Validate each on the 821-day combined set with v7-10 as the control; score on intraday floor / % days IF>=0 / uncovered-at-close first, total second.

1. **Give-up that chases (escalating give-up).** Not an open filter — the single biggest lever in this data. 17 uncovered losers = -$8,903, more than the whole two-week loss on opens. 13 of them had a give-up working whose limit sat a median $1.85 under the cover mark; paying the mark at the first give-up would have locked -$3,058 instead of -$6,628 (DERIVED from real marks). Arms: walk the give-up limit toward the mark over N minutes, cap the extra loss at e.g. $100/$200/$300 per 10W. (The 10/15 give-up-geometry sweep tested tent vs keep-strikes and 0 vs 5% — not a chasing give-up.)
2. **No adding to an underwater side (F16).** 17 opens removed (4 good, 5 give-up, 8 uncovered losers), delta +$4,288; same side already on and losing is the 15m/60m trend telling us the turn already happened. The backtest can model this exactly (held positions + spot). Also try F17 (any same-side uncovered): 21 opens removed (6 good, 6 give-up, 9 uncovered losers), delta +$4,763.
3. **No new opens late (F1 after 15:00 / F2 after 14:30).** 6 opens removed (1 good, 0 give-up, 5 uncovered losers), delta +$1,961. Mechanical: a give-up needs time and liquidity to fill; 5 of 6 post-15:00 opens ended uncovered. Sweep the cutoff 14:30/14:45/15:00/15:15. Proxy cannot see it (60-min window), so only the backtest can price it.
4. **Chase filter on the 60m band (F3 / F24).** F3 (flip/trend-cont with the 60m close already beyond its band in the trade direction): 26 opens removed (11 good, 10 give-up, 5 uncovered losers), delta +$2,324. F24 (any rule, with the 60m trend, 60m %B side >= 0.9): 43 opens removed (20 good, 10 give-up, 13 uncovered losers), delta +$6,012 — big delta but it also removes many good opens; the edge is tail-avoidance, not win rate (signal proxy W% 43% (83/195) removed vs 41% (97/235) kept). Backtest thresholds 0.9/1.0/1.1 and a "60m WWW" variant (F5: 9 opens removed (1 good, 3 give-up, 5 uncovered losers), delta +$2,668).
5. **15m %B chase (F4).** 42 opens removed (15 good, 17 give-up, 10 uncovered losers), delta +$4,544; the only filter where the signal-level proxy agrees clearly (W% 37% (83/225) removed vs 47% (97/205) kept). Overlaps F3; test separately then combined.
6. **Bull-flip skip (F11).** 25 opens removed (8 good, 11 give-up, 6 uncovered losers), delta +$3,149. Rule-level: trend-flip->bull is the worst rule in the sample. Plausibly sample-specific (several up-trend tops); backtest before believing.
7. **10:30-11:30 window (F21).** 20 opens removed (5 good, 10 give-up, 5 uncovered losers), delta +$2,958; the proxy agrees (W% 28% (15/54) removed vs 44% (165/376) kept) — the first-hour move often reverses there. Test as "require 60m agreement in this window", not a blanket ban.

Not worth building from this data: **F7 (skip opens against the 60m trend — the stated hypothesis)**: 30 opens removed (11 good, 18 give-up, 1 uncovered losers), delta -$1,068; fades against the trend lost often but cheaply (give-ups filled at ~-$50) and the winners paid for them. Repeated fades after a same-side give-up (F25): 18 opens removed (6 good, 11 give-up, 1 uncovered losers), delta +$291. Daily-band filter (F18): only 3 opens. "Should have waited 1-3 bars": the re-test rate on DIDNT opens is lower than on good opens, i.e. no evidence that waiting rescues a wrong-side open.

Combined (first-order, path effects ignored): C6 = F1 + F16 20 opens removed (5 good, 5 give-up, 10 uncovered losers), delta +$4,824; C2 = F1 + F3 + F16 41 opens removed (15 good, 13 give-up, 13 uncovered losers), delta +$5,943.
